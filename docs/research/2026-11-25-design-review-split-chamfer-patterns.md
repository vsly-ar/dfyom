# Design review: Split section, Chamfer inspector, Pattern inspector

Status: draft (review report, 2026-11-25; proposals P1–P8 done the same day, P3/P6/P8 provisional on Q130–Q132) · Task: [hardening U9](../plans/hardening.md) batch 1 (like v0.4 L2) · Against: [ui.md](../conventions/ui.md) ([Field](../conventions/ui.md#field), [Split part](../conventions/ui.md#split-part-split-parts-4a), [Chamfer inspector](../conventions/ui.md#chamfer-inspector), [Pattern inspector](../conventions/ui.md#pattern-inspector-p1)), the [design system page](../conventions/ui-design-system.html) and the user's direction (serious desktop CAD look, neutral chrome, sparse accent, purposeful motion)

The Split section (4a–4c), the loop chamfer additions (E1–E4b) and the Pattern inspector (P1–P4) landed without a visual review. This batch looks at them in the built app, in both themes, and fixes what is clearly off; what needs the user's taste is listed as proposals.

## How it was looked at

A throwaway Playwright spec on the e2e fixture (windows hidden, `DFYOM_HIDE_WINDOWS=1`) built the scenes through the bridge and the UI: the enclosure fixture ([`split.fixture.ts`](../../src/core/design/split/split.fixture.ts)) with no split, a level, dowels, two lines and a level (8 pieces), Puzzle keys, the Test fit… and Split to fit… menus, library-stock blanks and a piece; chamfers on the outline (Top / Bottom edge), on a drilling and on a pocket with an island (Loops, Corner lift); a pattern with the default, a preset, Polar with a shape boundary and Clip, Invert, Chamfer, Through and a stacked pattern. Each state was captured at 1440 × 900 (some at 1024 × 720) in dark and light (`data-theme`), before and after the fixes. The screenshots are not in the repo (scratch folder of the review session); the names below are theirs, `-dark` / `-light` appended.

## Proposals done (2026-11-25)

On the user's direction (serious desktop CAD look, neutral chrome, sparse accent, rich motion) all eight proposals are built. P1, P2, P4, P5 and P7 are plain fixes. P3, P6 and P8 are matters of taste and wait for the user's answers (**Q130** switch rows, **Q131** a shorter Split section, **Q132** compact piece chips); each is one commit, so reverting one is cheap. Decided along the way: a selected pattern has **no** amber fill at all (not even on hover of a cell: a click selects the whole pattern, so a per-cell highlight would promise something it does not do); the joint settings open when the joint is chosen in the inspector and are collapsed whenever a part with that joint is shown again. The rules are in [ui.md](../conventions/ui.md) ([Field](../conventions/ui.md#field), [Split part](../conventions/ui.md#split-part-split-parts-4a), [Chamfer inspector](../conventions/ui.md#chamfer-inspector), [Pattern inspector](../conventions/ui.md#pattern-inspector-p1)); the after-screenshots were captured again with the same throwaway spec (scratch folder, not in the repo).

## Checked and fine

- **Tokens and themes:** both themes read; no ad-hoc colours; the split preview, the cut planes (dashed `--vp-edge`, the accent only while focused, hovered or held: `split-02-level-focused`) and the exploded pieces follow the tokens. Danger buttons (Remove split) use the danger text in both themes.
- **Motion:** the pieces part over `--motion-slow`; nothing else animates beyond the shared components.
- **Keyboard:** every control is reachable by Tab; the level field's focus highlights its plane; the menus (Test fit…, Split to fit…, blank menu, Presets) open and close with the keyboard (Escape).
- **Units and field order:** lengths carry the project unit, angles °; field order follows ui.md in all three inspectors.
- **Truncation:** long navigator rows ("Pattern 1 · staggered · circle") and the part's stock line truncate with an ellipsis; the inspector wraps.
