# Design review of the v0.4 UI (L2)

Status: draft (review sent 2026-11-26; the convention and bug fixes R5, R7, R10, R11 and R14–R21 are done, marked "Fixed"; the taste items and R6 wait for the user's answer; answered in Q131 (D75): each item says what was decided and done) · Plan: [v0.4-llm-first.md L2](../plans/v0.4-llm-first.md#8-ui) · Judged against: [vision.md](../vision.md), [ui.md](../conventions/ui.md) (decision 18: amber used sparingly, never a large fill; [colour rules](../conventions/ui.md#colour); [motion](../conventions/ui.md#motion)), [inspector-layout.md](../conventions/inspector-layout.md) (S1–S6: group structure, field order, static notes as (i))

This review covers U1–U4, L1, the hardening UI of DF115–DF120 and W5/W6. It lists issues only; it changes nothing. **Answer per item** ("R3 no", "R8 yes but keep the prisms in light", "all S yes"). Sizes: **S** ≤ half a day, UI only · **M** about a day, or touches a doc decision · **L** several days or core work.

## Captures

The captures are hidden-window PNGs at the main window's default size (1440 × 900; the Shape Editor window at 1180 × 760), light and dark. They are in [`design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/`](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/). To render them again:

```sh
npm run build && DFYOM_REVIEW_DIR=design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4 npx playwright test e2e/design-review-v0.4.e2e.ts
```

The spec ([`e2e/design-review-v0.4.e2e.ts`](../../e2e/design-review-v0.4.e2e.ts)) only captures. It is skipped without `DFYOM_REVIEW_DIR`, so `npm run e2e` doesn't run it. Each link below opens the dark capture; the light one has the same name with `-light`.

## Top issues

The five most important issues, in order:

1. **R14:** the Bolt hole's Type card uses a large amber fill, against decision 18. On one inspector screen there are five amber fills.
2. **R5/R6:** the deviation region reads "Over-cut 2.5 mm · Facing 1 … unexplained". It names a cause and also says "unexplained", and it joins the facing and both pockets into one region.
3. **R1/R2:** at 16 px the vise looks like the block. Spoilboard and fixture plate share one icon, and so do tape, vacuum and glue.
4. **R8:** toe clamps are drawn as table-high prisms. In light they are heavy solids that hide the stock and the toolpaths.
5. **R20:** the QuantityInput adjust panel opens again when the field loses focus inside a modal dialog.

## Surfaces

### L1: workholding icons in the Add menu

[l1-workholding-add-menu-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/l1-workholding-add-menu-dark.png)

**Works:**

- All icons use the same outline weight, so the menu reads as one family.
- The accent is kept to the strap, jaws and plate parts, so the menu stays calm.

**Issues:**

- **R1 (S): at 16 px, Vise and Block look alike.** Each is a rectangle with a smaller box attached. The vise's two jaws merge into one blocky shape. Recommendation: redraw the vise as a U, two jaws standing up with the stock between them, taller than the block, plus a short screw handle on one side. Keep the block as one low solid against the stock, without the inner rectangle.
- **R2 (M, optional): Spoilboard and Fixture plate share the `base` icon, and Tape, Vacuum and Glue share `surface-hold`.** This was decided in L1 by kind group, but in the Add menu it gives two and three identical icons in a row. Recommendation: keep the shared base drawing and add a small distinguishing mark for each kind: holes for vacuum, a drop for glue, a strip for tape, a bolt-hole dot for the fixture plate. Or accept it, since the labels differ.
  - **Decided by the user** (Q131, D75, TG #396: yes, kind marks). **Done** 2026-11-26: the shared drawings stay; `fixture-plate` adds two bolt-hole dots in the plate, `tape` a strip under the stock, `vacuum` three holes, `glue` a drop; the spoilboard keeps the plain `base` ([ui.md § Icons (workholding, offset, deviation)](../conventions/ui.md#icons-workholding-offset-deviation)). The Add menu, the setup's rows and the Library window's rows use them.
- **R3 (S): the Screw icon reads as a mushroom or umbrella at 16 px.** The pan head is wider than the stock band, and the thread tick is lost. Recommendation: a narrower head, the shank clearly through the stock line, and two thread ticks.

### U1, W6: Arrange automatically, four toe clamps, an open clamp

- Arrangement: [w6-arrange-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/w6-arrange-dark.png)
- Refusal without toolpaths: [w6-arrange-refused-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/w6-arrange-refused-dark.png)
- Open item: [u1-clamp-item-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/u1-clamp-item-dark.png)

**Works:**

- The item rows read well: icon, name in medium weight, then the muted one-line summary ("left Y25 grip 10 top Z25").
- The refusal is a single plain line under the buttons that says what to do.
- The open clamp's keep-out halo is visible without using the accent.

**Issues:**

- **R4 (S): "Arrange automatically" reuses the toe-clamp icon.** Beside Add, it looks like a fifth item row. Recommendation: a ghost text button without an icon, or an "auto-arrange" glyph (four corner ticks around a box).
  - **Decided by the user** (Q131, D75): the text-only ghost button. **Done** 2026-11-26.
- **R8 (M): the clamps are drawn as prisms from the table to their top (Z25), 65 mm tall here.** In light they are the heaviest objects in the scene. They hide the stock sides and part of the toolpaths, and a toe clamp doesn't look like one. Recommendation: draw a toe clamp as its strap (a slab at the top height over grip + reach) with a thin stud. Lower the fill opacity in light (about 35 %, like the dark result). Keep the full prism only for the open item's keep-out.
- **R12 (M): an open clamp is six bare stacked fields, about 420 px tall.** Other inspectors give their geometry fields illustrations (Position, Depth, the hole's Section). Grip, Reach and Top above Z0 have none, although they are the hardest to understand. Recommendation: a small front-view illustration of the clamp, like the hole's section, that highlights the focused dimension. Then drop "Name" into the row title, renamed in place.
  - **Decided by the user** (Q131, D75, TG #396: "when creating icons for the fields, make them more or less generic so they can be reused somewhere else too, but different from existing ones. E.g. I don't like that 'width' icon everywhere illustrates a slot"). **Done** 2026-11-26: a set of generic dimension field illustrations (`length`, `width`, `height`, `topZ`, `depthBelow`, `thickness`, `overlap`, `overhang`, `opening`; `diameter` kept): the grey object and the amber extent between two end ticks beside it. `length` and `width` are redrawn, so every generic length and width (rectangles, shapes, machine axes, …) drop the slot; the slotted hole's Overall keeps the slot as `slotLength` ([ui.md § Field](../conventions/ui.md#field)). The workholding fields in the setup inspector and the Library window take them. An open toe clamp shows a front view (stock edge, strap, heel, stud and nut, at true proportions, `SketchView` as the hole's section) whose focused Grip, Reach or Top above Z0 is amber with its value; Width and Position along the edge lie across the view and have no part. The name is renamed in place in the open item's row (no in-place rename existed yet: a borderless field over the row toggle, Enter or leaving commits, Escape restores) ([ui.md § Workholding](../conventions/ui.md#workholding)).
- **R13 (M): right after "Arrange automatically", Facing 1 goes from 1 to 5 warnings.** These are the expected "uncut under Toe clamp n" findings, but four new warnings straight after an automatic action read as if the action failed. Recommendation: a result line under the buttons ("4 toe clamps placed; the facing leaves the area under them uncut"), and one grouped finding per operation instead of one per clamp.
  - **Decided by the user** (Q131, D75). **UI part done** 2026-11-26: after Arrange automatically a muted line under the buttons, "4 toe clamps placed." from the set result, with "; Facing 1 leaves the area under them uncut." once the setup's `plan.workholding.unmachined` findings name the operations; the next workholding edit clears it. The grouped finding is core work, done separately.

(see DF119 below), and the Work zero hint. Recommendation: move each to the (i) of its field (Type, Purpose, Work zero). Boxes stay for findings only.
  - **Fixed** (L2 fixes): Tape's note is the Type field's (i), the tap-drill reason the Purpose field's (i) (while a head disables Tap drill), the Work zero hint the Work zero group's (i); only the state note "On Hole 1; …" stays under Work zero Y ([ui.md](../conventions/ui.md)).

### U4: deviation panel

[u4-deviation-panel-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/u4-deviation-panel-dark.png)

**Works:**

- The statistics are a clean two-column facts list with tabular numerals.
- The swatch, together with the words "Over-cut", carries the meaning without relying on colour alone.
- The `deviation` icon on the heading and the button ties it to the compare legend.

**Issues:**

- **R5 (S): "Over-cut 2.5 mm · Facing 1 … unexplained" contradicts itself.** The row names the operation that made the region, then says "unexplained". "Unexplained" means only that no known cause applies (tool radius, workholding, hole mouth …), but next to an operation name it reads as "we don't know where this comes from". Recommendation: say "no known cause" in the muted cause slot. Keep the medium-weight emphasis for regions over 0.5 mm, worded "check: no known cause". Rename the summary's "1 unexplained" to "1 without a known cause".
  - **Fixed** (L2 fixes): the cause slot says "no known cause", a region over 0.5 mm without a cause "check: no known cause" (medium weight), the summary "1 without a known cause" ([ui.md § Deviation panel](../conventions/ui.md#deviation-panel)).
- **R6 (M, core V3): the facing's 0.5 mm and the two pockets' 2.5 mm come back as one region**, credited to Facing 1 with the pockets' worst value. This happens because the over-cut cells touch, so the region grouping joins them across operations and depths. Recommendation: split regions by operation (the cell's last cutting operation) or by deviation band before ranking. With this fixture the list would then show "Over-cut 0.5 mm · Facing 1" and two "Over-cut 2.5 mm · Pocket n" rows. The fix is in `src/core` (V3 regions), outside this review's lane. **Fixed 2026-11-26:** over-cut regions split by the operation that last cut each cell ([deviation.md § Implementation (V3)](../modes/deviation.md#implementation-v3)); U4's block now gives Facing 1 at 0.5 mm and each pocket at 2.5 mm.
- **R7 (S): at 900 px the panel sits under the Plan checks**, and its first region row is cut off at the window's bottom. The eight statistics rows come before the regions, which are what the user acts on. Recommendation: order the result as summary → regions → "Statistics" in an accordion (closed by default, summary "max 2.5 mm · 95 % 2.5 mm"). Scroll the group into view when a result arrives.
  - **Fixed** (L2 fixes): the result reads summary → regions → a closed "Statistics" accordion (summary "max … · 95 % …"); a result that arrives scrolls the group into view ([ui.md § Deviation panel](../conventions/ui.md#deviation-panel)).

### U3: Offset dialog, offset item, pocket Scale

- Row menu: [u3-shape-row-menu-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/u3-shape-row-menu-dark.png)
- Dialog: [u3-offset-dialog-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/u3-offset-dialog-dark.png)

- Pocket Scale: [u3-pocket-scale-dark.png](../../design/reviews/2026-11-26-v0.4/u3-pocket-scale-dark.png)

**Works:**

- The dialog is small and clear: one sentence, Distance, Direction, then the action.
- The offset item reads "Offset 3 inward · Shape 1", and the read-only Source has the dashed read-only look.
- The Scale tip explains the anchor.

**Issues:**

- **R20 (S): the QuantityInput adjust panel opens again on blur inside modal dialogs** (known). `e2e/shape-editor.e2e.ts` works around it with Tab. The hidden capture doesn't reproduce it, because a hidden window has no OS focus and the focus trap doesn't run its return. The code path: `onBlur` closes the panel, then Radix's FocusScope moves the focus back to the field, and `onFocus` opens the panel again (only `returning` suppresses that). Recommendation: open the panel on pointer down or on a keyboard focus that arrives from another control, not on a focus with no `relatedTarget` that comes back within one frame of the field's own blur. Add a component test inside a `Dialog`.
  - **Fixed** (L2 fixes): a focus with no previous element within 50 ms of the field's own blur (a focus trap's return) no longer opens the adjust panel; a click or a focus from another control still does. `QuantityInputDialog.test.tsx` reproduces it inside a `Dialog`; the Tab workaround in `e2e/shape-editor.e2e.ts` is gone ([ui.md § Field](../conventions/ui.md#field)).
- **R17 (S): Scale shows a bare "1.5"** beside "50 mm" fields. Recommendation: the suffix "×" in the unit slot (as "mm" is shown), so "1.5 ×".
  - **Fixed** (L2 fixes): a scale factor shows "×" in the unit slot (`QuantityInput` `unitLabel`; the pocket/profile Scale and the reference Scale) ([ui.md § Quantity input](../conventions/ui.md#quantity-and-expression-input)).
- **R21 (S): in the Shapes dialog, the row's menu opens over the dialog's footer** and hides its Done button. Recommendation: the menu opens at the pointer (context menu) or flips up when it would cover the footer.
  - **Fixed** (L2 fixes): a context menu opened in a modal dialog keeps above its footer, beside the pointer (shifts or flips up), so the Shapes row menu no longer covers Done ([ui.md § Row menu](../conventions/ui.md)).
