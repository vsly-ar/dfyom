# Inspector layout and value fields

Status: accepted (by delegation, D70, 2026-11-25; recommended options taken, the user may still revise) · Intake: [hardening DF104, DF105, DF110, DF111](../plans/hardening.md#intake-user-feedback-goes-here) · Parent: [ui.md § Field](ui.md#field), [§ Quantity and expression input](ui.md#quantity-and-expression-input), [§ Panels and inspectors](ui.md#panels-and-inspectors) · Look: [design system](ui-design-system.html)

How a value field picks its adjust steps, where explanatory text goes, what a click on a field's title row does, and how the right-hand inspectors are structured (groups, accordions, tabs). [ui.md](ui.md) keeps the general Field and QuantityInput rules; this doc owns the four topics below and the per-inspector target layouts. Contracts live in code once built (S1, S2 in the [slice plan](#slice-plan)); the code's header comments link back here.

The user's findings (TG #347, #349, 2026-11-25), short:

- **DF104** – preselect a sensible step in the adjust panel per field (facing depth → `.1`), scale the step list to the value and the stock (a 10.1 mm board needs no 10 and 100 mm depth steps), integer fields show integer steps only.
- **DF105** – static help text as an (i) icon by the field name with a hover tip, not printed under the input.
- **DF110** – re-organise the inspectors with groups, accordions and tabs; rarely used fields (rotation) in a collapsed accordion or a later tab; group titles more visible with spacing and separators.
- **DF111** – a click on a field's title row does not open the adjust panel.

## Audit (2026-11-25, main 83d3a59)

Generated from the code (a TypeScript-AST scan of every `QuantityInput` and its wrappers, throwaway, not committed) and from hidden captures of the inspectors of a test project (200 × 150 × 10.1 mm birch ply: facing, two pockets, outside profile with tabs, flat-bottom drilling, counterbored bolt hole, chamfer, 2 × 2 Clones, hex pattern; one plan). Findings that drive the design:

1. **Steps:** about 110 value-field call sites (≈ 60 direct `QuantityInput`s, the rest through `ParamField`, `ItemQuantity`, `StrategyQuantity`, `FeedField`, `CutDepthField`, library `QuantityField`). **None passes `step`**: every field opens on step `1` with the same six steps `.001 .01 .1 1 10 100` (`STEP_SIZES` in `QuantityInput.tsx`), whatever it measures – a 0.3 mm facing depth, a 24 000 rpm spindle speed, a 90° rotation, a tab count. Integer fields (tab count, clone columns/rows/count, polygon sides, flute count, axis steps) offer `.001` and `.01`, which only produce errors.
2. **Help text:** ≈ 160 places put a `note`/`info` line or a `note:` option under a field. Two thirds are static explanations ("How many positions across, along X, counting the one the row starts from.", "About the spindle axis; the work zero keeps its offset…", "Or enter the depth: the width follows through the angle.", the machine form's eleven Z-height and motion notes); the rest are value-dependent (provenance "From preset “Ply”." / "Default.", "Chosen automatically.", "Clamped to …", DFM and check findings). The Clones row form shows four lines of static text for four fields; the Setup inspector seven.
3. **Group titles** are `--text-xs` uppercase `--text-muted`, with `--space-2` around them, the same rhythm as a field label: in the captures a group title reads as one more label. Five separate implementations of a group: `Group` in `OperationFields.tsx`, local copies in `HoleInspector.tsx` and `PatternInspector.tsx`, `library/fields.tsx` (the only collapsible one, DF1), plain `<section>`+`h3` in Plan, Setup, Clones, Run. Two ad-hoc `<details>` (split settings, clone instances). `ui/Tabs` exists but no inspector uses it.
4. **Title-row click:** the label is a `<label htmlFor>`, so a click anywhere on the illustration or name focuses the input, and focus opens the adjust panel (DF9), which then covers the next field.

## Field step model (DF104)

### Step kinds

A field's steps follow **what it measures** (its step kind), not its quantity kind alone: a depth and a position are both lengths but want different steps. One table, keyed by step kind, holds every ladder; a field names its kind and nothing else.

| Step kind   | Used for                                                                                    | Ladder, mm projects (inch projects)              | Default step   | Cap (hide steps above)  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------- |
| `depth`     | depths, cut depth, breakthrough, levels in Z, clamp height, dowel hole depth, tab thickness | `.01 .1 .5 1 5 10` (`.001 .01 .05 .1 .5 1`)      | `.1` (`.01`)   | stock thickness         |
| `stepdown`  | stepdown, finish stepdown                                                                   | `.01 .05 .1 .5 1` (`.001 .005 .01 .05`)          | `.1` (`.01`)   | stock thickness         |
| `xy`        | positions, lengths, widths, diameters of shapes, spacings, margins, work zero, alignment    | `.1 .5 1 5 10 50` (`.01 .05 .1 .5 1 5`)          | `1` (`.1`)     | largest stock side      |
| `small`     | corner and fillet radii, chamfer width, slot and tab width, stepover, allowance, helix Ø    | `.01 .1 .5 1 5` (`.001 .01 .05 .1 .5`)           | `.1` (`.01`)   | largest stock side      |
| `fit`       | joint and dowel clearance, finishing allowance, deviation threshold                         | `.005 .01 .05 .1` (`.0005 .001 .005`)            | `.01` (`.001`) | –                       |
| `tolerance` | general tolerance, arc tolerance, junction deviation                                        | `.001 .005 .01 .05 .1` (`.0001 .0005 .001 .005`) | `.01` (`.001`) | –                       |
| `angle`     | chamfer, countersink, point, ramp angles                                                    | `.1 1 5 15`                                      | `1`            | –                       |
| `rotation`  | rotation of a shape, cell, reference, setup on the table                                    | `1 5 15 45 90`                                   | `15`           | –                       |
| `count`     | integers: tab count, clone columns/rows/count, polygon sides, flute count                   | `1 2 5 10`                                       | `1`            | the field's maximum     |
| `feed`      | feed and plunge rate, machine max feed, jog feed                                            | `10 50 100 500 1000` mm/min (`1 5 10 50` in/min) | `100` (`10`)   | the machine's max feed  |
| `rpm`       | spindle speed, min/max spindle speed                                                        | `100 500 1000 5000`                              | `1000`         | the machine's max speed |
| `percent`   | override sliders, stepover in % (if added)                                                  | `1 5 10 25`                                      | `5`            | –                       |
| `scale`     | reference scale, scalar factors                                                             | `.001 .01 .1 1`                                  | `.01`          | –                       |
| `machine`   | machine-form constants (steps per mm, acceleration)                                         | `.1 1 10 100 1000`                               | `10`           | –                       |

Rules:

1. **One table, keyed by step kind,** in `src/renderer/src/ui/QuantityInput/steps.ts` (pure, unit-tested; presentation only, so not in `src/core`): `STEP_LADDERS: Record<StepKind, { metric: readonly string[]; imperial: readonly string[]; default: …; cap?: CapSource }>` and `stepsFor(kind, { value, unitSystem, stock, max }) → { steps, initial }`. Ladders are decimal source strings in the field's display unit, so stepping keeps exact rationals (`stepRational`) and the unit as typed. `STEP_SIZES` and the `StepSize` type go; `QuantityInput` takes `stepKind` instead of `step`.
2. **Every value field names a step kind** (a type error without one, after S6); the [assignment table](#step-assignment-per-field) is the source of truth. A field the table does not fit takes the nearest kind; a new kind needs a row here first.
3. **Default (preselected) step:** the kind's default, lowered to the largest ladder step not above half the current magnitude (`|value| / 2`) when the value is smaller – a 0.3 mm facing depth opens on `.1`, a 3 mm pocket depth on `.1` (kind default), a 0.05 mm clearance on `.01`. An empty or zero value takes the kind's default. Never raised above the kind's default by a large value: big steps are one click away.
4. **Remembered step:** a step the user chooses in the panel is kept for that field kind and role (e.g. `pocket.depth`, `plan.feedRate`) for the rest of the session (renderer ui store, not saved); it wins over rule 3 while it fits the ladder after scaling.
5. **Scaling to the stock and limits:** a kind with a cap hides the steps larger than **half** the cap (the part's stock thickness for `depth`/`stepdown`, its largest side for `xy`/`small`, the machine's maximum for `feed`/`rpm`; no part or machine = no cap). A 10.1 mm board shows `.01 .1 .5 1 5` for a depth. At least three steps always stay (the smallest ones); never more than six.
6. **Integers** (`count`, and any field whose `validate` requires an integer): only integer steps; `Alt` (×0.1) on ↑/↓ does nothing; `Shift` (×10) stays. The ladder stops at the field's maximum (tab count ≤ 20 shows `1 2 5 10`, polygon sides ≤ 12 `1 2 5`).
7. **Labels** stay as today (`.1`, `1`, `10`); units are the display unit's, shown by the value line under the steps. The segmented control keeps its fixed cell widths so a shorter ladder does not jump the panel.
8. **Keyboard steppers** (`↑`/`↓` in the input) use the same preselected step, so ↑ on a facing depth adds 0.1 mm.

## Info tips (DF105)

1. **Static explanation → (i) tip.** Text that explains the field and does not change with its value goes into an (i) icon at the end of the title row (after the label text, before the lock column): what the field means, how it is measured, what "empty" means ("Optional: …", "Empty = through"), units and conventions ("Negative goes the other way."), and machine-form notes. One tip per field, one or two sentences; glossary terms keep their wording.
2. **What stays inline under the field:** the echo, errors, hard and soft warnings with their fixes, DFM findings, info notes about the current value (`Clamped to 3.00 mm…`, "Chosen automatically.", tool-choice reasons, "No value: enter one…"), the lock's disabled reason, read-only reasons. Rule of thumb: if the text would change when the value changes, it stays inline.
3. **Provenance moves to the title row:** "From preset “Ply”." and "Default." become a short muted tag at the title row's end ("Preset", "Default"; its tooltip the full sentence) instead of a line under the input.
4. **Look:** `info` icon, `--icon-sm` (16 px), `--text-muted`, `--text` on hover; no box; hit area 24 × 24 px (padding), centred on the label's line. Tip = the shared `Tooltip` (inverted surface, max 32 ch per line), side `top`, align `start`, opens after the Tooltip's delay, closes on leave.
5. **Keyboard and screen readers:** the (i) is not a Tab stop (Tab still goes field to field, DF9). The tip's text is in the control's `aria-describedby` (always, so a screen reader reads it with the field). For sighted keyboard users the tip also opens when the control gets **keyboard** focus (`:focus-visible`) and stays for 4 s or until typing starts; it sits above the title row so it never covers the adjust panel below. Pointer focus never opens it.
6. **No touch behaviour** (desktop app); no click toggling, a click on (i) does nothing beyond what hover does.
7. **Not for group explanations:** a group-level note ("This plan follows the design while it is a draft…") stays as one line under the group title, muted, or as the group's own (i) in its header when it is static.
8. **API:** `Field` and `QuantityInput` take `info?: ReactNode`; `FieldMessage` severities `note`/`info` stay for value-dependent notes only. Library `QuantityField`'s `note` option becomes `info`.

## Title-row click (DF111)

1. A pointer press on the title row (illustration, label text, empty space of the row, provenance tag) **focuses the input without opening the adjust panel**: the label stays a `<label htmlFor>` (screen readers and focus unchanged); a `pointerdown` on it sets the same "do not open on this focus" flag the panel uses when focus returns to the input (`returning` in `QuantityInput.tsx`).
2. A click **in the input** opens the panel as today (`onClick` → `openAdjust`), as do `⌘↓`/`Ctrl+↓`. Focus by **Tab** still opens it (DF9 unchanged).
3. A press on (i) neither focuses the input nor opens the panel.
4. Toggle fields (switch, segmented control) are unchanged: a click on their label still toggles where it did.

## Inspector structure (DF110)

### Building blocks

All in `src/renderer/src/ui/` (S2), replacing the five local group implementations and the ad-hoc `<details>`:

| Block            | Component                             | Use for                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Group**        | `InspectorGroup`                      | fields the user thinks of together (Shape, Depth, Tool); always open            |
| **Accordion**    | `InspectorGroup collapsible`          | a group whose fields are changed rarely; closed shows a one-line summary        |
| **Tabs**         | `ui/Tabs` inside the inspector body   | two or three distinct concerns in one long inspector (Plan op: Cut / Feeds)     |
| **Advanced row** | inside a group, `InspectorGroup.More` | one to three rarely used fields of an otherwise common group (Rotation, Anchor) |

### Group header style

- Title: `--text-xs`, uppercase, `--weight-bold`, letter-spacing 0.06em, colour **`--text`** (was `--text-muted`): it must not read as a field label.
- Separator: a 1 px `--border` rule above each group except the first, full inspector width (bleeds into the inspector's padding).
- Spacing: `--space-5` above the rule, `--space-3` from the rule to the title, `--space-3` from the title to the first field; fields inside keep their `--space-2` gap. The last group ends with `--space-4`.
- Optional at the right of the title: a count or state chip (`3 rows`, `2 errors`) in `--text-muted`, and the group's (i).
- Headings stay `h3` (`h4` inside a nested feature body) with the section's `aria-label` = title, so `getByRole('region', …)` / `group` queries keep working.

### Accordions

- Header = the group header plus a chevron (`chevron-down`, turned −90° closed) as a `button` with `aria-expanded` / `aria-controls` (as the library's DF1 group). Closed: the title, then `· summary` in `--text-muted` (the values that matter, e.g. "0° · Centre", "Defaults"), so nothing hides silently.
- Motion: height and opacity with `--motion-base` / `--ease-standard`; reduced motion = instant.
- **Never hides a problem:** a closed accordion with an error or warning inside shows its severity icon in the header and opens itself when that message's "show" or fix targets a field in it (the same applies to a field focused by pick, Find or an agent selection).
- **Remembered open state** per inspector kind and group id (`pocket.advanced`, `plan.strategy`), across selections and launches, in the renderer settings (beside the remembered window state, [ui.md § Remembered window state](ui.md#remembered-window-state-and-dialog-folders)). Not per item.
- **Collapsed by default:** a group whose every field is changed in under ~1 in 5 uses (from dogfood and the [audit](#audit-2026-11-25-main-83d3a59)): rotation, anchor, floor fillet, tab positions, strategy defaults, touch-off spot, plan copy of the design, machine motion. Everything a new item needs to be correct starts open.

### Advanced row ("More")

Inside an open group, rarely used fields go last behind a quiet text button "More (Rotation, Anchor)" that expands in place (same remembered state rules). Used when only one to three fields are rare, so a whole accordion would be heavier than the fields.

### Tabs

- Only when an inspector holds two or three distinct concerns **and** is longer than about two inspector heights at 800 px; at most three tabs; short labels (one word).
- The first tab holds what is changed on almost every edit. The tab strip sits right under the header, sticky while the body scrolls.
- **Remembered** selected tab per inspector kind (session and launches, as accordions).
- A tab with an error or warning in it shows a severity dot; selecting a message's target switches the tab first.
- Inactive tab panels stay **mounted but hidden** (`forceMount` + `hidden`), so field state and in-flight edits survive and tests can address them after selecting the tab.

### Field order rules

1. Within a group: the mode doc's parameter order ([design.md § Operations](../modes/design.md#operations-v01)); position before size before orientation; each axis pair X then Y.
2. Groups: **what** (shape / position / type) → **how deep** → **tool** → refinements (corners, tabs, chamfer) → rarely used (accordions) → findings last.
3. Read-only derived values (Chamfer's Diameter) sit after the fields they come from.

## S1 implementation notes

- **Steps:** `ui/QuantityInput/steps.ts` holds the ladder table and `stepsFor` (rules 3–6); `QuantityInput` takes `stepKind`, `stepMax` (count fields: Columns/Rows 100, sides and flute count 12), `stepUncapped` (setup clearance/retract/clamp heights may exceed the stock) and `stepKey` (the remembered-step key; default `${stepKind}:${label}`, operation fields use `<op kind>.<path>`). Caps come from `StepScaleProvider`: the Design inspector gives the part's stock, the Plan inspector the plan part's stock and the machine snapshot's max XY feed and spindle speed.
- **Remembered step:** the ui store (`steps`, `rememberStep`) through `StepMemoryProvider` in the shell; outside a shell (tests, gallery) a module-level session map.
- **Every app value field has a `stepKind`** already (Design and Plan inspectors, library forms, Stock dialog, Reference panel/tools, Shape Editor, Settings snap grid, gallery). The prop stays optional with the `xy` fallback; making it required is all that is left of S6's step work.
- **(i) tip:** the icon is `aria-hidden` and not a Tab stop; its text is a hidden span in the control's `aria-describedby`. Keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`, not after a pointer press) opens it for 4 s or until a key other than Tab/Shift. Static hints moved into tips in this slice for every inspector (not only in S3–S6): Chamfer (4), Clones row hints, Pattern (2), Split (3), Reference panel/tools (4), Setup rotation, Aid size hints, Workholding margin, library tool/machine/stock notes. - **Provenance tag:** `tag {text, title}` on the title row; the text is drawn by CSS (`attr(data-text)`) so the label stays the control's accessible name, the title is a tooltip and part of the description. Feed fields tag preset, calculator and strategy sources; strategy parameters tag "Default". An entered value keeps its inline "Entered by you." line with the Use default / recommendation fix, since that line carries an action.
- **DF111:** the label's click is prevented and focuses the input the way Escape-from-panel does (no panel); the doc's pointerdown flag was not needed.

## S2 implementation notes

Built 2026-11-25 (provisional, awaits the user's review of the captures). Chrome and consolidation only: no field moved between groups, no group became an accordion that was not one (S3–S6 do that).

- **One group component:** `ui/InspectorGroup` (`InspectorGroup.tsx`, `.module.css`) draws the [header style](#group-header-style). The rule above every group but the first is `.group ~ .group` (sibling groups only) and bleeds `--inspector-bleed` into the container's padding: `--space-3` by default (the inspectors' `.fields`), `0` for `h4` groups and in library forms. The first group gets the `--space-5` above it too when fields precede it (Name), just no rule. Nested `h4` group titles are `--text-muted`, so the hierarchy reads (Part (plan copy) › Shape; Sizes › Defaults). Props: `title` (the region's name, always), `heading` (shown instead, e.g. "Split · 2 pieces", "Line 2"), `level` 3/4, `meta` (chip at the end), `actions` (buttons at the end, Shape Editor), `info` (the group's (i), in the section's description), `ref` and section attributes (`data-*`, `aria-busy`, `tabIndex`).
- **Accordion:** `collapsible`; the whole title line is the disclosure button (chevron, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`); closed it shows `· summary` and, with `severity`, the problem's icon (named "has errors"/"has warnings"). Open state: controlled (`open`/`onOpenChange`), remembered (`rememberKey`), or local; `defaultOpen`; `forceOpen` shows it open while true (library tool Details while a field inside is invalid). Motion: the body's height (`interpolate-size`) and opacity over `--motion-base`, the chevron turns; a zero `--motion-base` (reduced motion, or tests without tokens) makes it instant.
- **Closed content stays mounted, hidden** (as tab panels): field state survives, and `revealInInspector(el)` (a bubbling DOM event) opens every closed accordion and More row around `el` and selects its tab synchronously, so the caller focuses it next ("never hides a problem"). The bolt hole's Defaults uses it for a head-sink target; message targets in S3–S6 call it before focusing.
- **More row:** `InspectorGroup.More` (`fields` names the button "More (Anchor, Rotation)", same open-state props); in the gallery and tests only until S3.
- **Tabs:** `ui/Tabs` keeps every panel mounted (`forceMount` + `hidden`), takes `severity` per item (a dot, ", has errors" for screen readers), `rememberKey` and `sticky`; a revealed field in a hidden panel selects its tab. No inspector uses tabs yet (S5, S6).
- **Remembered state:** the app settings `inspectorGroups` (key → open) and `inspectorTabs` (key → tab), merged by key ([ui.md § Settings](ui.md#settings)); the shell provides them (`InspectorMemoryProvider`), without a shell a session memory (tests, gallery).
