# Construction surfaces

Status: accepted, implemented ([§ Implementation (V1)](#implementation-v1), [(V2)](#implementation-v2), [(V3)](#implementation-v3), [follow-ups](#implementation-follow-ups)) (by delegation, 2026-11-26: the user delegated draft confirmation, D70, so the recommended options below are **accepted by delegation (D70, 2026-11-26)** and the user may veto; flagged items are listed under [Flagged](#flagged-for-the-user)) · Hardening [DF118](../plans/hardening.md#intake-user-feedback-goes-here), user TG #369 · Research and reproduction: [2026-11-26-construction-surfaces.md](../research/2026-11-26-construction-surfaces.md) · Builds on: [design-v0.2.md § Surface table](design-v0.2.md#surface-table-per-direction), [geometry.md § Evaluation checks](../architecture/geometry.md#evaluation-checks), [design.md § Ordering and composition](design.md#ordering-and-composition) · Terms: [glossary.md](../glossary.md)

The user asked (TG #369): "if I wouldn't leave a small rim inside the 'Pocket for M5', it would show an error that this reference surface for 'M5 long hole' and 'Chamfer 1' no longer exists (because it was later removed by the chamfer). I guess a virtual surface should remain to be referenced against. Probably as a product of just the operation 'Pocket for M5' against the surface it references. And up the dependency tree to the stock face."

A **construction surface** is a surface of the [surface table](design-v0.2.md#surface-table-per-direction) that later cuts have removed from the finished part. References still resolve to its level: the producing operation applied to the surface it stands on, back to a stock face. That has always been how levels are computed. The [research](../research/2026-11-26-construction-surfaces.md#summary) found that only the mesh-based `dfm.surfaceMissing` check and the mesh-based pick glyphs treated a consumed surface as gone. This doc makes the term explicit, narrows the check and shows the surface.

## Resolution

Unchanged, and now a stated rule:

- A surface's level is `on.level − depth` (floors, flat bottoms), or the tab rule for tab tops, from its operation and its `on` surface, recursively down to a stock face ([design-v0.2.md § Surface table](design-v0.2.md#surface-table-per-direction)). Tolerances propagate as before.
- A reference is **valid** when the table defines the id for an earlier operation (`checkReferences`, `design.referenced`), and the operation is not disabled ([design.md § Disabling](design.md#disabling-operations-and-features-df106)). Whether the finished part still has triangles of that surface does not matter.
- **Construction surface** (derived per evaluation): a surface that is referenced (by `on`, `edgeOf`'s face or a formula), is defined by the table, and has no triangles in the evaluated part. A surface that keeps any triangles is an ordinary surface. Partial removal is never reported (as today).

## Check: `dfm.surfaceCutAway`

Replaces `dfm.surfaceMissing` (code, message and test; warnings are derived and never stored, so nothing persists the old code).

- **Dependants of a surface S:** the operations whose `on` is S, or whose formula references S, or whose `edgeOf` operation is a dependant, closed transitively over the [dependency graph](design.md#ordering-and-composition) (`designGraph` plus `edgeOf` and `on`). Owned operations of features count like any other (a hole's countersink standing on its counterbore floor).
- **Consumers of a construction surface S** (owner O, level ℓ, patch P = the face's region at ℓ: a pocket's shape region, a facing's stock face rectangle, a flat drilling bottom's circle, a tab's rectangle): the operations after O whose removal solid intersects the slab P × [ℓ − δ, ℓ] (in O's face frame, δ = 0.001 mm) by more than ε_V. Candidates are filtered by bounds first. This runs only for surfaces that lost all triangles, which is rare, so the evaluation budget is unaffected (measured in V1).
- **Rule:** if every consumer of S is a dependant of S, there is no warning (the user's case: the slot and Chamfer 1 both stand on the recess floor). Otherwise there is one `dfm.surfaceCutAway` per referencing operation and parameter (as today's check), `severity: 'warning'`, `params: { surface, by: [non-dependant consumer ids] }`. Message: "It starts from or refers to Facing 1 floor, which Facing 3 cuts away. Its level still holds, so the cut is measured from a surface the part no longer has. Check the depth or start from another surface."
- **Why keep a warning at all:** an unrelated later cut that erases the datum means the referencing cut's visible depth no longer matches its entered depth. For example, a pocket 5 mm deep from Facing 1's floor is only 3.3 mm deep after a later 2 mm facing. A dependant that cuts away its own face, such as a countersink or a stepped pocket of the same outline, keeps the intent.
- Geometry warnings order, merging and `design.warning.list` behave as for the old check ([design.md § DFM warnings](design.md#dfm-warnings-task-28-wiring)); the row in [geometry.md § Evaluation checks](../architecture/geometry.md#evaluation-checks) is updated in V1.

## UI

- **`On` field and formula references:** when the chosen surface is a construction surface, the field's support area shows a `note` (the standing-note style of design review U9, [ui.md](../conventions/ui.md)): "Cut away by Chamfer 1 · still measured from its level". The (i) tip explains a construction surface in one sentence. A `dfm.surfaceCutAway` warning replaces the note on its field.
- **`On` select:** construction surfaces stay listed (they already are, from the table) with a muted "(construction)" suffix.
- **3D view:** while an operation whose `on` is a construction surface is selected, or while pick mode offers one, the patch P is drawn at its level as a dashed outline in `--vp-selected` with a 12 % fill, and no depth write. That is the CAD datum-plane look, drawn from the evaluation's `construction` patches, not from the mesh. Pick mode places its normal glyph at the patch centroid, so it can be picked like any face. Under reduced motion it appears without the fade.
- **Hover and selection** of a construction surface in the On select highlight the same outline (the navigator lists operations, not surfaces, so it has nothing to hover; [follow-ups](#implementation-follow-ups)).
- **Edge chamfers** have no `On` field (their face follows the edge): the note sits at the **Edge** field and names the face, "Pocket for M5 floor is cut away by M5 long hole · still measured from its level", with the same (i) tip. The viewport's text summary names it ("Pocket for M5 floor, cut away by Chamfer 1, shown as an outline.").

## Plan

- **No change to toolpaths or levels:** the plan reads levels from the surface table ([research § Where references are resolved](../research/2026-11-26-construction-surfaces.md#where-references-are-resolved-today)).
- **Z reference:** a construction surface is not a touch-off surface. The existing `cutAway` rule already refuses it with its own message ([z-reference.md](z-reference.md)). Its candidates list is unchanged.
- **Plan copy of the design** and plan operations' Design tab show the same note as the design inspector, from the evaluation of the plan's own part (the snapshot scene's), so it matches the plan copy even where plan-only edits differ from the design.

## Agent API

- No new tool and no new arg: `tools/list` is unchanged (measured in V2: 111,990 chars of 120 K).
- The evaluation outcome (`design.part.evaluate` via `job_wait` / `job_get`) and `design.warning.list` gain `construction: [{ surface, consumedBy: string[], referencedBy: [{ operationId, param }] }]` (output schema only; empty when none).
- `dfm.surfaceCutAway` replaces `dfm.surfaceMissing` in warnings. The primer ([`primer.ts`](../../src/core/agent/primer.ts)) gains one sentence: "A surface a later cut removes still resolves at its level (a construction surface); stand on it freely."
- `design.measure` `faceCentre` does not target construction surfaces (there are no triangles). Its `near` search is unchanged.

## Persistence and migration

Nothing is stored. `construction` is derived per evaluation, like warnings. No `schemaVersion` bump and no migration ([migrations.md § Pre-alpha](../conventions/error-handling/migrations.md#pre-alpha-exception-no-data-preservation-yet)). Projects such as clamps_40 can drop the 0.05 mm rim after V1.

## Risks

- **A silenced dependant consumption that was a mistake:** a chamfer far too wide eats its floor and nothing warns. Mitigation: the operation's own checks still run (`dfm.chamferTooDeep`, corner and fit checks), the note is visible on the `On` field, and the outline shows in 3D.
- **Coincident planes in the slab test:** a consumer that starts exactly at ℓ (the chamfer's cone top) touches the slab only on its face. Taking the slab below ℓ (δ) and the ε_V threshold make touching not count. V1 tests the clamps case, a same-outline stepped pocket, and a facing erasing a floor.
- **Cost:** the slab intersections run only for referenced surfaces with no triangles. V1 measures a 100-clone part in which every clone consumes its floor, against the evaluation budget ([apply.md](../architecture/apply.md)).

## Implementation (V1)

Done 2026-11-26.

- **Dependants:** `surfaceDependants` ([`surfaces.ts`](../../src/core/design/surfaces.ts)) closes `operationReferences` (`on` and formula references) and `edgeOf` over the operations in one pass in design order (a dependency always points to an earlier operation). The mapper (`toGeometryInput`) adds `PartGeometryInput.dependants` for the referenced surfaces that have any; the field is left out otherwise. A facing's or profile's stock-face `on` is not a reference ([design-v0.2.md](design-v0.2.md#surface-table-per-direction)), so a facing is never a dependant of the stock face it removes.
- **Check:** [`construction.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/construction.ts) `constructionSurfaces`, run by `evaluatePart` after `geometryChecks` (warnings keep the table order). The patch P is the owner's triangles of that role (removal meshes: the material is in front of the face; cut, retained and stock meshes: behind it). The slab is P extruded δ = 0.001 mm into the material as one closed mesh. Candidates are the later operations whose bounds overlap the slab's. **Each consumer takes what the earlier ones left** (intersect, then subtract), so a removal's overshoot above its own face, which only crosses material an earlier cut took, never counts (the drillings on a facing floor are not consumers of the stock top). A consumer counts above `CONSTRUCTION_OVERLAP` = ε_V · δ (an overlap area of 0.001 mm²); a cut that only touches P from above removes ≈ 0.
- **Lone or last consumer:** the face has no triangles, so later cuts took the whole slab. The last candidate gets what the others left without a boolean (one volume) when its bounds reach past the slab; a candidate that only touches P still runs the boolean. A single consumer therefore costs no boolean.
- **Result:** `PartEvaluation.construction` (`ConstructionSurface` in [`model.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/model.ts)): `surface`, `consumedBy` (operation order), `referencedBy` (`operationId`, `param`), `patch` (`direction`, face-frame `z`, boundary loops of `[u, v]` in the face frame of the surface's direction). `dfm.surfaceCutAway` has `params: { surface, by }` and the renderer message of [§ Check](#check-dfmsurfacecutaway) ([`messages/dfm.ts`](../../src/renderer/src/messages/dfm.ts)). `dfm.surfaceMissing` is gone from the code list; the agent tools' output schemas carry the new code (V2 measures `tools/list`).
- **Tests:** `design/construction.test.ts` rebuilds clamps_40 through the design commands (chamfer 2.85 mm: the rim keeps the floor, nothing reported; 3.2 mm: no warning, `construction` lists `pocket1.floor` consumed by the slot and the chamfer, patch 11.3 mm wide at z −2.3 on `-z`), a 2 mm facing after a pocket on facing 1's floor (`dfm.surfaceCutAway` by `facing2`) and a stepped pocket of the same outline (silent). `checks.test.ts` covers the geometry level (stock top cut away by the facing; silent with dependants; nothing when triangles remain).
- **Cost** (`npm run perf`, `evaluate.perf.ts`, Apple M1 Pro class, medians of 11 interleaved runs): 100 cloned recesses whose floors are each consumed by a slot and a stepped pocket of the same outline (two consumers, one boolean pair each): 360–420 ms without references, +190–280 ms with the 100 construction surfaces (≈ 2–3 ms per surface with two or more consumers). With one consumer per floor the added time is within the run-to-run noise (± 5 ms). A part with a few construction surfaces (clamps_40: one) stays within the 50 ms warm budget ([apply.md](../architecture/apply.md)); the 100-clone part is above it with or without the check.

## Implementation (V3)

Done 2026-11-26 ([ui.md § Construction surfaces](../conventions/ui.md#construction-surfaces-df118)).

- **Data:** the renderer reads the consumers from `design.warning.list` (`DesignWarnings.construction`, `OperationFieldsContext.construction`) and the patches from the evaluation's mesh push (`EvaluationMesh.construction`, left out when empty), so the outline always matches the drawn mesh.
- **Fields:** `OnField` shows the note "Cut away by Chamfer 1 · still measured from its level" and the (i) tip (`Field.info`) when its surface is a construction surface; the operation itself is left out of the names (the slot's note names Chamfer 1; "Cut away by this operation" when it is the only consumer). A `dfm.surfaceCutAway` on the field replaces the note. A formula field (Depth and the other length fields) referring to one shows "Pocket for M5 floor is cut away by … · still measured from its level". The `On` list suffixes construction surfaces "(construction)". Helper: `constructionAt` in `modes/design/model.ts`. An edge chamfer has no `On` field (its face follows the edge); its note came with the follow-ups.
- **3D view:** `constructionOverlays` (`modes/design/construction-view.ts`, pure: three's `ShapeUtils` triangulates the loops, holes by winding) maps the patches to the part frame; `ConstructionLayer` draws the dashed outline (`--vp-selected`, on top) and a fill at `MIX.pickEligible` (12 %, `MIX.pickHover` when hovered) without depth writes, fading in (none under reduced motion). Shown for the selected operation's (or feature's operations') referenced construction surfaces as a read-out, and in pick mode for the eligible ones, pickable, with a normal glyph at the patch centroid. In pick mode the part mesh lets the pointer through a dimmed surface, so an outline behind material can still be hovered and clicked. The viewport summary names it ("Pocket for M5 floor, cut away by M5 long hole and Chamfer 1, shown as an outline.").
- **Follow-ups:** done, [§ Implementation (follow-ups)](#implementation-follow-ups).
- **Tests:** `construction-view.test.ts` (fill with holes, bottom-face level and glyph, glyph off a hole); `Construction.test.tsx` (the slot's note and tip, the outline's level and summary, "(construction)" in the list, pick mode offering the floor with a glyph, a click setting Spot's `On`); `e2e/construction-surfaces.e2e.ts` builds the clamps part with the recess centred, checks the note, views from below and picks the outline in the built app (renders `construction-{note,pick}-{light,dark}.png` with `DFYOM_RENDERS=1`, reviewed in dark).

## Implementation (follow-ups)

Done 2026-11-26 (decisions taken by the implementing agent under D70, the user may veto).

- **Edge chamfer note:** the chamfer's `on` (its edge's face) is a reference, so `constructionAt(…, 'on')` finds it. The closest spot is the **Edge** field (the face is where the edge sits; there is no `On` row and the Side field shows only for through profiles). The note names the face (`noteOf`, as formula fields do), since the Edge field does not show it, and the field gets the (i) tip. A `dfm.surfaceCutAway` on the chamfer's `on` replaces the note. A countersink's owned chamfer (read-only) shows it too.
- **On-list highlight:** `Select` gained `onHighlight` (the option Radix focuses under the pointer or the arrow keys; `null` when the pointer leaves the options or the list closes; no `null` between two options). `OnField` writes it to the ui-store's `surfaceHighlight` (view state; cleared when the field unmounts). The `Viewport` takes it as `highlight`: it feeds the same `SurfaceState.hovered` a pointer hover sets (`--vp-hover` on an ordinary surface; the pointer's own hover wins), and the construction layer's `hovered`, so a construction surface's outline turns hot (pick-hover fill, thicker line). `useDesignScene` adds the highlighted construction surface to the read-out overlays when it is not shown already (not pickable), and the viewport summary names it while it shows. **Navigator:** it lists operations, not surfaces, so there is no surface hover there; nothing added. The plan snapshot scene draws no construction outlines, so the plan copy's `On` list sets the highlight but the plan viewport ignores it.
- **Plan copy note:** `PlanCopyGroup` passes `construction` from `usePlanEvaluation(plan.id)` (the plan part's mesh push, which already carries `consumedBy` and `referencedBy`), not from `design.warning.list`: the plan copy can differ from the design. Last good evaluation, like the snapshot scene's mesh. `plan.warning.list` is unchanged (no agent API change).
- **Tests:** `Construction.test.tsx` (Chamfer 1's Edge note and tip; hovering "Pocket for M5 floor (construction)" in Spot's `On` list adds its outline, hot, and the mesh hover state, Escape drops both), `PlanCopy.test.tsx` (the On note in the plan copy from a pushed plan evaluation), `e2e/construction-surfaces.e2e.ts` (Edge note on Chamfer 1; the list hover adds the outline to the viewport summary and closing removes it; renders `construction-{edge,hover}-{light,dark}.png`, reviewed in dark).
