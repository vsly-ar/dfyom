# Plan mode v0.3: toolpaths for general loops

Status: accepted (user, D29 of 2026-11-22: T-A to T-F and the S4 questions as recommended, [Decisions](#decisions)) · Design: [design-v0.3.md](design-v0.3.md) · 2D layer: [architecture/shapes.md](../architecture/shapes.md) · v0.1 strategies and generators: [plan-v0.1.md](plan-v0.1.md#pass-strategies-v01) · Setups: [plan-v0.2.md](plan-v0.2.md) · Post: [grbl.md](../architecture/grbl.md) · Tasks: [plans/v0.3.md](../plans/v0.3.md) · Terms: [glossary.md](../glossary.md)

The v0.1 generators compute offsets of rectangles and circles analytically ([plan-v0.1.md § Toolpath generators](plan-v0.1.md#toolpath-generators-task-210)). v0.3 feeds them **regions and paths of lines and arcs** and takes every offset from the 2D port ([shapes.md § 2D port](../architecture/shapes.md#2d-port)). The [toolpath data model](plan-v0.1.md#toolpath-data-model), the job, the cache, setups and the export stay; the common rules (clearance, retract plane, levels, plunge rate, arcs ≤ 180°) apply unchanged.

## Decisions

Settled with the user when this doc was accepted together with [design-v0.3.md](design-v0.3.md#decisions) (D29, 2026-11-22: every recommendation taken).

| Id  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T-A | Pockets of any region keep **offset clearing** (rings from the inside out, a finishing ring per loop); adaptive clearing stays later.                                     |
| T-B | Toolpaths keep **arcs exact**: every arc of an offset is an `arc` move, exported as G2/G3. Lines are never fitted to arcs or the reverse, except degenerate arcs.         |
| T-C | Profiles roll round convex corners of the tool-centre path (arc joins, the CAM "round corners" default); sharp (extended) corners are later.                              |
| T-D | Finishing passes enter and leave on **tangent arcs** (lead-in / lead-out arcs) where there is room, else straight as v0.1.                                                |
| T-E | An `on` groove of an open path is cut **zigzag by level** (along, then back one level lower); climb or conventional does not apply to a full-width slot.                  |
| T-F | Plan-only edits may change a shape operation's placement, shape reference and parameters, not shape definitions.                                                          |
| T-G | **No gap rings** in v0.3: the step is clamped to R; gap rings (or adaptive clearing) are the later route to larger steps ([Pocket](#pocket-offset-clearing-of-a-region)). |
| T-H | A **clamped stepover is shown** as an info note on the operation, with the effective stepover.                                                                            |
| T-I | **Blocked links retract** (retract, rapid, plunge); routing them through the cleared area is later.                                                                       |

## Pocket: offset clearing of a region

Strategy parameters as [v0.1 pocket](plan-v0.1.md#pocket-offset-clearing); no new ones.

Spike S4 measured this strategy on S1's corpus and this section takes its recommendations: [research/s4-offset-clearing.md](../research/s4-offset-clearing.md).

- **Rings:** the roots are the components of `offset(region, R + finishAllowance)`; each node (one component: an outer ring and its island rings) has as children the components of **its own** offset by the step, until empty. Offsets split at necks and merge with islands, so the rings form a **tree** and steps restart per branch; no rule places the innermost ring.
- **Step:** equal steps ≤ **min(`stepover`, R)**; a larger stepover is clamped to R (0.5 D) for offset clearing and the operation shows an info note with the effective stepover (T-H). Why: a point of a node outside its child lies less than the step from the node's ring, so with steps ≤ R every ring's sweep covers it and clearing is complete for any region. Any larger step leaves material in **bands** (where a node is between 2R and 2·step wide, e.g. between an island ring and the wall ring) and at **new corners** where merging fronts or splits sharpen a child ring; v0.1's √2·R cap left up to 54 mm² on S4's star. The default stepover (0.4 D) is below R, so nothing changes by default. **Gap rings** (a ring round each piece the larger step misses) are the later route to larger steps (T-G). The heightmap simulation is the test oracle for coverage ([Tests](#tests)).
- **v0.1 rectangles and circles:** if T1 replaces the v0.1 pocket generator for them too, their cap drops from √2·R to R for stepovers above 0.5 D; v0.1 goldens use the default stepover and are unaffected. Otherwise they keep the v0.1 rule.
- **Order:** per branch of the tree, from the innermost ring outwards (post-order, v0.1 order), climb direction (outer loops counter-clockwise, island loops clockwise, seen from the spindle). Children are visited nearest-first.
- **Links:** a straight feed move to the next ring only when every point of it lies inside the **parent ring's region** (all its children are cut, so that region is cleared to within a step of its ring); otherwise retract, rapid on the retract plane and plunge (re-enter; routing through the cleared area is later, T-I).
- **Entry** per leaf, with v0.1's fallback order: `helix` at the leaf's deepest point (the centre of the largest circle inside the leaf ring's region, found by offsetting until empty) if the helix + 2a fits inside the **root's tool-centre region** (not only the leaf's); `ramp` **along the leaf ring** (lapping as needed) at `rampAngle`; else `plunge`.
- **Retracts:** expected count per level is **one per leaf after the first plus one per blocked link** (golden-file check).
- **Finishing:** one ring per loop (outer and every island) at offset R, levels of `finishStepdown`, lead-in and lead-out arcs from the cleared side (T-D).
- **Rest material** in necks narrower than the tool is not cut; design warns `dfm.restMaterial` ([design-v0.3.md § DFM](design-v0.3.md#dfm-checks-for-shapes)), Plan repeats it as `plan.restMaterial` (warning) for the plan's own tool. Rest machining with a smaller tool is later.

### Implementation (T1, region-level generator)

Done before K3, not yet wired: [`src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.ts) `generateRegionPocket(input, port, steps?) → AppResult<Toolpath>`, pure, with the `Region2dPort` passed in (the worker's `TaskControl.region2d()` when wired). The input is a `GeneratorInput` whose operation is `{ region, onZ, depth }`: a normalised region already placed in the setup's work plane (WCS XY, mm); strategy and cutting values as the v0.1 pocket, the same input checks (`model.invariant`, `plan.check` with `toolTooLarge`, `noFlat`). Ring tree, link test and the 2D-loop-to-`Seg` conversion: [`region-rings.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-rings.ts); rest material: [`rest-material.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/rest-material.ts). Choices the bullets above leave open:

- **Step:** `clearingStep` = min(`stepover`, flat radius R − RE); for a flat end mill that is min(`stepover`, R), for a bull nose the flat radius as v0.1's floor-coverage rule. `stepoverClamped` tells the wiring when to add the T-H info note.
- **Roots** per piece of the wall's tool-centre region `offset(region, R)`: its offset by `finishAllowance`; a piece too narrow for the allowance is roughed at the wall itself and gets no finishing ring (v0.1's clamped allowance). A tool up to `TOOL_FIT_TOLERANCE` wider than the region is accepted (offset by R − 0.01 mm), as v0.1.
- **Links:** the straight-link test is exact – the link's hits with the node's rings split it into stretches, and each stretch's midpoint must lie in the node's region (its boundary included, so a link along the ring just cut is allowed); the spike sampled every 0.1 mm. Blocked links and the first ring of a node reached without a link: retract, rapid, plunge at `plungeRate`.
- **Entry:** the helix is v0.1's (full turns from its front point at ≤ `rampAngle`, one flat circle) around the leaf's deepest point (offsets bisected to 1e-4 mm); after it the leaf's ring starts at its point closest to the helix centre, so the link stays inside the bore. The ramp walks the leaf's outer ring at exactly `rampAngle` (`walkRing`); the flat lap at the level is the ring itself. Entries start at the previous level, as v0.1.
- **Finishing:** each loop starts at the middle of its longest line (else its longest arc); the lead arcs are quarter circles of radius R, R/2 or R/4 – the first whose circle lies in the wall's tool-centre region and whose ends lie in the roughed region – else straight leads from the roughed ring (v0.1), else none. The pocket keeps "no new parameters", so R stands in for the profile's `leadRadius` default (0.5 D). Between finishing levels the tool feeds from the lead-out end back to the lead-in start through the lead circle.
- **Rest material:** `findRestMaterial(port, region, R)` → pieces `{ area, at }` of `region − opening(R)` minus corner zones, above 0.5 × R² ([shapes.md § Parameter checks](../architecture/shapes.md#parameter-checks-for-shapes)). A corner zone is a disc of radius R / tan(θ/2) (+1 %) round a convex vertex of interior angle θ, or round the tangent intersection of a convex arc of radius < R (θ = π − sweep; U-turns over 170°, e.g. a slot end, get none). The opening is `offset(offset(region, R), −R)`. Registering `plan.restMaterial` and the T-H note in `plan.warning.list` is wiring (they need the shapes in the design snapshot).
- **Input mapping (K3):** the design side delivers the operation: [`outline.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/outline.ts) `pocketRegion(op)` gives `{ region, onZ, depth }` in the operation's face frame (numbered loops, absolute `[u, v]`); the wiring hands it to `generateRegionPocket` in the face frame and the setup's WCS turn is applied to the toolpath, as for the v0.1 inputs ([Wiring](#wiring-t1-t2)).
- **Not switched:** v0.1 rectangles and circles keep `generatePocket` (√2·R cap); the wiring step kept them ([Wiring](#wiring-t1-t2)).
- **Tests** ([`region-pocket.test.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.test.ts)): ring tree (splits, merges with islands, offset oracle per child, step cap), the generic rules on every case (no feed move outside the wall's tool-centre region, so links never cross islands), retracts per level = leaves − 1 + blocked links (S4's pocket with 2 islands: 5 + 2), helix, ramp (8 mm slot), plunge, lead arcs tangent per loop, conventional direction, errors and abort; the heightmap oracle ([`testing/region-coverage.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/testing/region-coverage.ts), S4's classification) on the star with 2 islands, the pocket with 2 islands at a clamped 0.9 D, the X0 enclosure cavity (Béziers flattened to lines until I1) and the 4 mm neck and 30° triangle: no gouge, no leftover, uncut = region − opening within 1 %. Goldens, the 30 random regions and the virtual-machine streaming stay with T3. Perf ([`region-pocket.perf.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.perf.ts)): the star with 4 levels and finishing in ≈ 9 ms (budget 500 ms), rest material ≈ 3 ms.

## Profile on general loops

Strategy parameters as [v0.1 profile](plan-v0.1.md#profile-with-tabs), plus `leadRadius` (length, default 0.5 D, ≥ 0; 0 = straight lead) for the finishing pass.

- **Passes:** distances from the shape as v0.1 (part side at R + allowance, then toward the offcut in steps ≤ `stepover`); each pass is `offset(loop, ±distance)` (outside: grows; inside: shrinks; an inside pass that vanishes in a neck splits, and each piece is cut).
- **`on`:** passes symmetric about the centreline; a slot equal to D is one pass on the loop or path. Closed loops keep the profile's climb direction for the outer passes; open paths zigzag by level (T-E) with ramps along the path.
- **Entry:** ramp along the first pass as v0.1; on an open path the ramp zigzags along the first stretch.
- **Tabs:** as v0.1 (lift where the tool disk overlaps a nominal tab band), with the general bands of [shapes.md](../architecture/shapes.md#geometry-for-shape-operations) at the design's positions; boundaries by sampling and bisection along arcs as along lines.
- **Input (K3):** `profileChain(op)` (the loop or open path in the face frame) and `tabStretches(op)` (tab stretches along it from its start point; none on an open path or a blind groove) in [`outline.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/outline.ts).
- **Finishing:** the part-side pass at full depth, lead-in and lead-out arcs of `leadRadius` on the offcut side, starting and ending at the start point unless a tab is there (then mid-way to the next tab).

### Implementation (T2, region-level generator)

Done after K3's mapping, not yet wired: [`src/core/toolpath/region-profile.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-profile.ts) `generateRegionProfile(input, port, steps?) → AppResult<Toolpath>`, pure, `Region2dPort` passed in as T1. The operation is `{ chain, closed, side, slotWidth, onZ, depth, through, tabs: { positions, width, thickness } }`: `chain`/`closed` from `profileChain(op)` turned into the setup's WCS (a mirroring turn that makes it clockwise is fine), `positions` = `tabCentres(length, op.tabs)` (the centres of `tabStretches`), `through` = no `op.depth`. The strategy is the v0.1 profile's plus `leadRadius`. Tab bands: [`region-tabs.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-tabs.ts); the tab lift is v0.1's `liftOverTabs`. Choices the bullets above leave open:

- **Spacing:** `profileStep` = min(`stepover`, R) (a blind groove: the flat radius), so passes cover any loop by the pocket's argument; v0.1's analytic shapes allow 2R (√2·R inside rectangles). `profileStepClamped` tells the wiring when to add the T-H note. Pass distances as v0.1: `rowPositions(R + allowance, max(R, slotWidth − R))` from the offcut side.
- **Direction:** the region-on-left loops of each offset, reversed for climb when the part lies inside that offset (outside passes, the inner side of an `on` loop, its centreline), for conventional otherwise (inside passes, the outer side of `on`, the rings round an open path) – v0.1's rule (outside climb clockwise, inside climb counter-clockwise) for any loop orientation.
- **`on` closed:** per level the centreline, then pairs at ±d (offset ±d of the loop) out to slotWidth/2 − R − allowance; finishing rings at both walls. **`on` open:** the centreline from the end nearest the tool, alternating per level without a retract; a wider slot adds closed rings round the path (`offsetPath` by d, retract after the level) and finishing ring(s) of the band. The ramp walks the path from the end at `rampAngle`, turning back at the ends as often as needed (`zigzag`), then returns flat to the end and cuts the whole path at the level, so the level is complete without a clean-up pass. Ramps need a path or ring ≥ D long, else plunge (as T1).
- **Links** between rings: a straight feed inside the roughing's tool-centre band (T1's exact test), else retract, rapid, plunge (T-I). Closed profiles retract after each level (v0.1).
- **Inside covering slot:** if `offset(loop, slotWidth)` is empty the slot covers the opening: passes beyond the loop's inradius are empty and skipped, no tabs (v0.1). A piece too narrow for the allowance is roughed at the wall without finishing (v0.1 clamp); an inside tool up to `TOOL_FIT_TOLERANCE` too wide is accepted.
- **Tabs:** the band of a stretch is built per element (a quad across the slot for a line, an annular sector for an arc, a sector at each corner where the normals diverge), not bent on the medial axis: near concave corners closer than the slot width it reaches past the "nearest point on the stretch" rule – G1 should build the same band (or both move to one helper). Tool disk test exact against these line/arc loops; lifts by v0.1 sampling and bisection. Start point: arc length 0 unless a tab (width/2 + R) reaches it, then mid-way to the next tab.
- **Leads:** quarter circles of `leadRadius`, then ½, ¼, tangent at the start point on the offcut side, accepted when 16 samples of each lie in the slot's tool-centre region; else straight leads of the allowance; else none. Lead-in and lead-out use the tangents at the ring's start and end (a ring may start at a corner).
- **Errors:** `model.invariant` for the chain (`checkChain` rules, `noArea`, `empty`), `openNeedsOn`, `onlyOn` (blind not `on`), `tabsNotAllowed`, `tabOutside`, `tabsOverlap`, tab thickness `range`, the v0.1 numeric rules and `leadRadius` ≥ 0; `plan.check` `toolTooLarge` (slot or opening), `noFlat` (wide blind groove without a flat).
- **Tests** ([`region-profile.test.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-profile.test.ts)): pass distances by offset, inside neck split, direction (climb/conventional, clockwise chain), ramp/plunge, lead arcs tangent and fallbacks, tabs by arc length on lines and arcs (lift spans width + D on a line), `tabCentres` count tabs, `on` closed and open (zigzag, short-path zigzag ramp, wide slot rings), errors, abort; the heightmap oracle (`regionCoverage` with tab cells) on the X0 enclosure outline (imported by `importSvg`, outside with 3 tabs and finishing), its cavity (inside, uncut = slot − swept within 1 %), the bracket outside/inside/`on` with tabs and an open-path blind groove: no gouge, no leftover, tabs kept. Perf ([`region-profile.perf.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-profile.perf.ts)): the outline outside 14 mm slot with 4 tabs and finishing ≈ 18 ms, `on` 12 mm ≈ 19 ms (budget 500 ms). Goldens with T3.
- **Not switched:** v0.1 rectangles and circles (`outside` / `inside`, `count` tabs) keep `generateProfile`; the wiring step kept them ([Wiring](#wiring-t1-t2)).

### Wiring (T1, T2)

The plan side of both generators ([`toolpath/inputs.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/inputs.ts), [`request.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/request.ts), [`plan/shape-findings.ts`](../../src/core/plan/shape-findings.ts)):

- **Which generator.** `toGeneratorInputs` sends an operation that `isAnalyticOperation` accepts (a rectangle with rotation 0 or a circle; a profile `outside` / `inside`, through, with `count` tabs) to the v0.1 generator, so v0.1/v0.2 plans keep byte-identical toolpaths and G-code (the goldens are unchanged; the input mapping is already byte-identical, [shapes.md § Implementation (K3)](../architecture/shapes.md#implementation-k3)). Every other pocket or profile – named or rotated shapes, `on`, blind, tab positions – becomes a `regionPocket` / `regionProfile` request. Decision of the wiring step: keep v0.1 for rectangles and circles; moving them (cap R instead of √2·R / 2R, lead arcs, other ring order) would change every v0.1 golden for no gain at the default stepover.
- **Frame.** The region (`pocketRegion`) or chain (`profileChain`) stays in the operation's face frame, like every other generator input; a rotated setup turns the finished toolpath (`wcs` on the request, `turnToolpath`, arc sense kept), so the mapping is the v0.1 `toWcs` turn. A mirrored shape is already placed (and renumbered) by K3, so the generator gets a valid loop either way.
- **Profile operation** (`regionProfileOperation`): `onZ` 0 (the stock face), `depth` = the blind groove's depth, else the stock thickness with `through`; tab centres = `tabCentres(length, op.tabs)` on a closed through loop only (`tabStretches`' rule), else none. `leadRadius` has no plan parameter yet: the documented default 0.5 D.
- **Worker.** The request schemas carry the region/loop (`RegionSchema`, `LoopSchema`); `generateRequest(request, steps, region2d)` takes the worker's `TaskControl.region2d` for the two region kinds and fails with `internal.unexpected` without it. Steps and abort are the generators' own (`op.aborted` at the next step).
- **Findings** (`plan.warning.list`, with `services.region2d`): `plan.restMaterial` (warning, `field: tool`, `params { operation, area, u, v }` in the face frame) for every piece `findRestMaterial` finds in a general pocket with the plan's tool radius; `plan.stepoverClamped` (info, T-H, `field: stepover`, `params { operation, stepover, effective }`, "stepover clamped to 3 mm (half the tool) for offset clearing of pocket1") when `stepoverClamped` / `profileStepClamped` clamp the resolved stepover. Plain v0.1 shapes get neither. Neither blocks generation.
- **Shape checks in plan commands** (K3 known issue): `planFindings`, `planOperationInputs` (generate, get, export, the apply job, pending), the stock re-import and the revert now pass the port to the part check (`checkPart` in `plan/validity.ts`), so `profileOneLoop`, `tabOutside`, `tabsOverlap` and `tabsFit` run there as in the design. The rebuild and blocker checks (`build.ts`, `rebuildBlocker`, `libraryUpdateBlocker`) still run without it.
- **Stale.** The toolpath `inputHash` already hashes the plan's whole part, shapes included. `plan.stale.list` now marks an operation `changedInDesign` when the definition of the named shape it places differs between the plan's taken part and the design (a shape-only edit); `partChanged` lists `shapes` as before.
- **Tests** ([`plan/shape-toolpaths.test.ts`](../../src/core/plan/shape-toolpaths.test.ts)): a named-shape pocket and an `on` profile with tab positions generate through the job and export with G2 and G3 of ≤ 180°; `plan.restMaterial` in a 4 mm neck and both clamped-stepover notes at a 4.5 mm stepover, not blocking; a shape-only edit makes the draft's toolpath stale and a settled plan's operation `changedInDesign`; abort at a step; the X0 drawing (`importSvg`, loops turned into `path` items) pocketed and profiled with 3 tabs through plan export.

## Arcs in, G2/G3 out

- Every offset element is a `line` or `arc` move; arcs over 180° are split (v0.1 rule). Helices (ramp and helix entries along arcs) are `arc` moves with a Z change.
- **Degenerate arcs:** sweep < 1e-4 rad → `line`; radius < 0.005 mm → `line` (the offset of a corner arc just above zero). There is no radius rule in the toolpath: a huge radius is not a deviation bound (a 225 m arc over 300 mm bows 0.05 mm, [shapes.md § Arc fitting](../architecture/shapes.md#arc-fitting)).
- **Huge-radius arcs:** whether a G2/G3 of huge radius goes out as `G1` (GRBL computes in float32, so km radii lose precision in `I`/`J`) is the **post's** decision, by the arc's sagitta against the post's own tolerance, beside its short-chord rule ([grbl.md § Output dialect](../architecture/grbl.md#output-dialect)); added to the post with these goldens.
- **GRBL arcs:** the post already writes arcs as GRBL reads them (`I`/`J` from the written start, short chords as `G1`, the `error:33` end-on-circle guard, [grbl.md § Output dialect](../architecture/grbl.md#output-dialect)). v0.3 changes nothing there; it feeds the post many more arcs, so the post's property tests gain the shape goldens (tiny corner arcs, near-180° splits, helices along arcs) in mm and inch.

## Plan side

- **Design snapshot:** carries the part's shapes; plan operations of shape operations group as today (equal holes only). Plan-only edits per T-F through `plan.design.operation.update`.
- **Tool checks** ([plan-v0.1.md § Tool assignment](plan-v0.1.md#tool-assignment-and-checks)): "wider than the pocket / slot / opening" uses the region's width (`offset(region, R)` non-empty); a corner radius below the tool radius is a warning (the corner keeps the tool radius), not an error, as today.
- **Order checks** (`plan/order.ts`) use the placed region's bounding box where they used the shape's.
- No change to setups, export per setup, Run.
