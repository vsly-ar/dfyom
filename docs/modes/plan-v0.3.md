# Plan mode v0.3: toolpaths for general loops

Status: implemented (v0.3; accepted by the user, D29 of 2026-11-22: T-A to T-F and the S4 questions as recommended, [Decisions](#decisions)): the region generators ([T1](#implementation-t1-region-level-generator), [T2](#implementation-t2-region-level-generator)), their [wiring](#wiring-t1-t2) and the [tool and order checks, goldens and heightmap tests](#implementation-t3) (T3); the exit part's programs are golden through the agent tools (X1) and the UI (X2); not built: [Later](#later) · Design: [design-v0.3.md](design-v0.3.md) · 2D layer: [architecture/shapes.md](../architecture/shapes.md) · v0.1 strategies and generators: [plan-v0.1.md](plan-v0.1.md#pass-strategies-v01) · Setups: [plan-v0.2.md](plan-v0.2.md) · Post: [grbl.md](../architecture/grbl.md) · Tasks: [plans/v0.3.md](../plans/v0.3.md) · Terms: [glossary.md](../glossary.md)

The v0.1 generators compute offsets of rectangles and circles analytically ([plan-v0.1.md § Toolpath generators](plan-v0.1.md#toolpath-generators-task-210)). v0.3 feeds them **regions and paths of lines and arcs** and takes every offset from the 2D port ([shapes.md § 2D port](../architecture/shapes.md#2d-port)). The [toolpath data model](plan-v0.1.md#toolpath-data-model), the job, the cache, setups and the export stay; the common rules (clearance, retract plane, levels, plunge rate, arcs ≤ 180°) apply unchanged.

## Decisions

Settled with the user when this doc was accepted together with [design-v0.3.md](design-v0.3.md#decisions) (D29, 2026-11-22: every recommendation taken).

| Id  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T-A | Pockets of any region keep **offset clearing** (rings from the inside out, a finishing ring per loop); adaptive clearing stays later.                                     |
| T-B | Toolpaths keep **arcs exact**: every arc of an offset is an `arc` move, exported as G2/G3. Lines are never fitted to arcs or the reverse, except degenerate arcs.         |
| T-C | Profiles roll round convex corners of the tool-centre path (arc joins, the CAM "round corners" default); sharp (extended) corners are later.                              |
| T-D | Finishing passes enter and leave on **tangent arcs** (lead-in / lead-out arcs) where there is room, else straight as v0.1.                                                |
| T-E | An `on` groove of an open path is cut **zigzag by level** (along, then back one level lower); climb or conventional does not apply to a full-width slot.                  |
| T-F | Plan-only edits may change a shape operation's placement, shape reference and parameters, not shape definitions.                                                          |
| T-G | **No gap rings** in v0.3: the step is clamped to R; gap rings (or adaptive clearing) are the later route to larger steps ([Pocket](#pocket-offset-clearing-of-a-region)). |
| T-H | A **clamped stepover is shown** as an info note on the operation, with the effective stepover.                                                                            |
| T-I | **Blocked links retract** (retract, rapid, plunge); routing them through the cleared area is later.                                                                       |

## Pocket: offset clearing of a region

Strategy parameters as [v0.1 pocket](plan-v0.1.md#pocket-offset-clearing); no new ones.

Spike S4 measured this strategy on S1's corpus and this section takes its recommendations: [research/s4-offset-clearing.md](../research/s4-offset-clearing.md).

- **Rings:** the roots are the components of `offset(region, R + finishAllowance)`; each node (one component: an outer ring and its island rings) has as children the components of **its own** offset by the step, until empty. Offsets split at necks and merge with islands, so the rings form a **tree** and steps restart per branch; no rule places the innermost ring. Offsets by discs compose exactly, so level k is computed as the root's offset by k × step and each component hung under the level-(k − 1) node that contains it: offsetting each level's own result compounded the 2D library's epsilon-sized debris over dozens of levels until an offset failed (LLM test F3).
- **Step:** equal steps ≤ **min(`stepover`, R)**; a larger stepover is clamped to R (0.5 D) for offset clearing and the operation shows an info note with the effective stepover (T-H). Why: a point of a node outside its child lies less than the step from the node's ring, so with steps ≤ R every ring's sweep covers it and clearing is complete for any region. Any larger step leaves material in **bands** (where a node is between 2R and 2·step wide, e.g. between an island ring and the wall ring) and at **new corners** where merging fronts or splits sharpen a child ring; v0.1's √2·R cap left up to 54 mm² on S4's star. The default stepover (0.4 D) is below R, so nothing changes by default. **Gap rings** (a ring round each piece the larger step misses) are the later route to larger steps (T-G). The heightmap simulation is the test oracle for coverage ([Tests](#tests)).
- **v0.1 rectangles and circles:** if T1 replaces the v0.1 pocket generator for them too, their cap drops from √2·R to R for stepovers above 0.5 D; v0.1 goldens use the default stepover and are unaffected. Otherwise they keep the v0.1 rule.
- **Order:** per branch of the tree, from the innermost ring outwards (post-order, v0.1 order), climb direction (outer loops counter-clockwise, island loops clockwise, seen from the spindle). Children are visited nearest-first.
- **Links:** a straight feed move to the next ring only when every point of it lies inside the **parent ring's region** (all its children are cut, so that region is cleared to within a step of its ring); otherwise retract, rapid on the retract plane and plunge (re-enter; routing through the cleared area is later, T-I).
- **Entry** per leaf, with v0.1's fallback order: `helix` at the leaf's deepest point (the centre of the largest circle inside the leaf ring's region, found by offsetting until empty) if the helix + 2a fits inside the **root's tool-centre region** (not only the leaf's); `ramp` **along the leaf ring** (lapping as needed) at `rampAngle`; else `plunge`.
- **Retracts:** expected count per level is **one per leaf after the first plus one per blocked link** (golden-file check).
- **Finishing:** one ring per loop (outer and every island) at offset R, levels of `finishStepdown`, lead-in and lead-out arcs from the cleared side (T-D).
- **Rest material** in necks narrower than the tool is not cut; design warns `dfm.restMaterial` ([design-v0.3.md § DFM](design-v0.3.md#dfm-checks-for-shapes)), Plan repeats it as `plan.restMaterial` (warning) for the plan's own tool. Rest machining with a smaller tool is later.

### Open sides (DF61)

Hardening DF61 (follow-up of DF53: a ring pocket ending on the stock edge kept the stock margin as a wall, so a margin strip narrower than the tool between a pocket and the stock edge was refused or left standing). A pocket wall on the stock edge or beyond it is air and does not bound the tool:

- **When.** A pocket (any shape, v0.1 rectangles and circles included) is **open** when part of its region, placed in its face frame, lies on the stock edge (within 0.001 mm) or beyond it ([`opensStock`](../../src/core/geometry/open-sides.ts)). A pocket wholly inside the stock keeps every wall (a wall 0.01 mm from the edge is a wall).
- **Open region.** The toolpath cuts the **open region** ([`openRegion`](../../src/core/geometry/open-sides.ts)) instead of the pocket region: the pocket region within the stock face plus the band within `reach` of it outside the stock (the shape's own outline beyond the stock is dropped: it only bounded air). Its tool-centre region keeps every point of the tool that is inside the stock within the pocket (the band lies wholly outside the stock), so real walls keep their offset and finishing ring, while along open sides the tool centre runs past the edge. `reach` = r (1 + √½) + finishing allowance (`openReach`): the tool centre goes up to r √½ + allowance beyond the edge, which clears the corner where a real wall meets an open side (the tool touches it from 45° below the edge); a wider band only adds air. An open pocket always goes to the region generator (an open v0.1 rectangle or circle too; closed ones keep the v0.1 generator and their goldens).
- **Checks.** With the stock known (`shapeMeasure(port, op, face)`: Design's DFM and tool fit, the auto tool choice, the plan's width, corner and helix checks) an open general-shape pocket fits every tool (the tool enters from beyond the edge: no `pocketSize` / `dfm.toolUnfit` width finding, `narrowest` = ∞), its corners on or beyond the stock edge are dropped (reached from outside; DF53's `openCorner` covers corners far enough outside a closed reading), and its rest material is that of the open region inside the stock (`dfm.restMaterial`, `plan.restMaterial`). v0.1 rectangles and circles keep their v0.1 rules (a rectangle's corner radius rule, a circle's diameter), except that a rectangle's corner on the stock edge or beyond it is open too (follow-up, [`rectangleWallCorner`](../../src/core/geometry/open-sides.ts)): the corner radius rule (the plan's `cornerRadius` check, the tool fit and auto choice, `dfm.cornerSharp` / `dfm.cornerTooSmall`) applies only while one of its corners – the middle of its corner arc, the vertex when sharp, the same point as a general shape's corner – lies inside the stock face more than 0.001 mm from the edge. A slot across the whole stock therefore takes any tool; a rounded end exactly on the edge keeps its arc inside the stock and stays a wall; the width rule stays the rectangle's own.
- **Travel and workholding.** No workholding model exists ([plan-v0.2.md](plan-v0.2.md#decisions) decision 1): like a facing's overshoot and an outside profile's slot, open sides assume nothing stands within r (1 + √½) + allowance (about 0.85 D) of the stock edge along those sides – the tool's edge reaches that far. Clamps belong on the stock's closed sides or outside that band; since DF59 (interim) the setup inspector, the `plan_get` summary and the gCode header say how far the tool reaches past each stock side ([plan-v0.2.md § Tool reach past the stock](plan-v0.2.md#tool-reach-past-the-stock-and-clamps-hardening-df59-interim)). Rapids and links stay at the setup's retract and clearance heights.
- **Enclosure.** In the agent's enclosure project the underside relief and both ring pockets (sides beyond the stock edge) and the cable notch and rim rebate on the top (sides on the edge) are open: their toolpaths now end r √½ + allowance past the edge instead of following the shapes' own outlines (relief 648 → 659 s, notch 28 → 31 s: the corners on the edge are cleared now).
- **Tests:** [`open-sides.test.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/open-sides.test.ts) (`rectangleWallCorner`; open/closed, the open region keeps the pocket inside the stock; heightmap oracle: a 2 mm margin strip round a part with a Ø6 tool cleared without touching the part, a notch on the edge with and without finishing: its corners with the real walls cleared), `plan/shape-checks.test.ts` (the margin strip takes the 6 mm tool, no width, corner or rest finding, toolpath past the edge; a ring 1 mm inside the edge still refuses it), `design/shape-dfm.test.ts` (DF53 ring corners now open, a 1 mm strip fits every tool); follow-up: `geometry/dfm.test.ts` (a sharp slot across the stock: no corner warning; one end inside: warns), `plan/shape-checks.test.ts` (the 6 mm tool on 1 mm corners: refused inside the stock and with ends on the edge, accepted across the stock).

### Implementation (T1, region-level generator)

Done before K3, not yet wired: [`src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.ts) `generateRegionPocket(input, port, steps?) → AppResult<Toolpath>`, pure, with the `Region2dPort` passed in (the worker's `TaskControl.region2d()` when wired). The input is a `GeneratorInput` whose operation is `{ region, onZ, depth }`: a normalised region already placed in the setup's work plane (WCS XY, mm); strategy and cutting values as the v0.1 pocket, the same input checks (`model.invariant`, `plan.check` with `toolTooLarge`, `noFlat`). Ring tree, link test and the 2D-loop-to-`Seg` conversion: [`region-rings.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-rings.ts); rest material: [`rest-material.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/2d/rest-material.ts) (shared with the design DFM since K4). Choices the bullets above leave open:

- **Step:** `clearingStep` = min(`stepover`, flat radius R − RE); for a flat end mill that is min(`stepover`, R), for a bull nose the flat radius as v0.1's floor-coverage rule. `stepoverClamped` tells the wiring when to add the T-H info note.
- **Roots** per piece of the wall's tool-centre region `offset(region, R)`: its offset by `finishAllowance`; a piece too narrow for the allowance is roughed at the wall itself and gets no finishing ring (v0.1's clamped allowance). A tool up to `TOOL_FIT_TOLERANCE` wider than the region is accepted (offset by R − 0.01 mm), as v0.1.
- **Links:** the straight-link test is exact – the link's hits with the node's rings split it into stretches, and each stretch's midpoint must lie in the node's region (its boundary included, so a link along the ring just cut is allowed); the spike sampled every 0.1 mm. Blocked links and the first ring of a node reached without a link: retract, rapid, plunge at `plungeRate` (below the first level: lower, [§ Low entries and links](#low-entries-and-links-df136)).
- **Entry:** the helix is v0.1's (full turns from its front point at ≤ `rampAngle`, one flat circle) around the leaf's deepest point (offsets bisected to 1e-4 mm); after it the leaf's ring starts at its point closest to the helix centre, so the link stays inside the bore. The ramp walks the leaf's outer ring at exactly `rampAngle` (`walkRing`); the flat lap at the level is the ring itself. Entries start at the previous level, as v0.1; the tool gets there by a rapid from 0.5 mm above it ([§ Low entries and links](#low-entries-and-links-df136)).
- **Finishing:** each loop starts at the middle of its longest line (else its longest arc); the lead arcs are quarter circles of radius R, R/2 or R/4 – the first whose circle lies in the wall's tool-centre region and whose ends lie in the roughed region – else straight leads from the roughed ring (v0.1), else none. The pocket keeps "no new parameters", so R stands in for the profile's `leadRadius` default (0.5 D). Between finishing levels the tool feeds from the lead-out end back to the lead-in start through the lead circle.
- **Rest material:** `findRestMaterial(port, region, R)` → pieces `{ area, at }` of `region − opening(R)` minus corner zones, above 0.5 × R² ([shapes.md § Parameter checks](../architecture/shapes.md#parameter-checks-for-shapes)). A corner zone is a disc of radius R / tan(θ/2) (+1 %) round a convex vertex of interior angle θ, or round the tangent intersection of a convex arc of radius < R (θ = π − sweep; U-turns over 170°, e.g. a slot end, get none). The opening is `offset(offset(region, R), −R)`. Registering `plan.restMaterial` and the T-H note in `plan.warning.list` is wiring (they need the shapes in the design snapshot).
- **Input mapping (K3):** the design side delivers the operation: [`outline.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/outline.ts) `pocketRegion(op)` gives `{ region, onZ, depth }` in the operation's face frame (numbered loops, absolute `[u, v]`); the wiring hands it to `generateRegionPocket` in the face frame and the setup's WCS turn is applied to the toolpath, as for the v0.1 inputs ([Wiring](#wiring-t1-t2)).
- **Not switched:** v0.1 rectangles and circles keep `generatePocket` (√2·R cap); the wiring step kept them ([Wiring](#wiring-t1-t2)).
- **Tests** ([`region-pocket.test.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.test.ts)): ring tree (splits, merges with islands, offset oracle per child, step cap), the generic rules on every case (no feed move outside the wall's tool-centre region, so links never cross islands), retracts per level = leaves − 1 + blocked links (S4's pocket with 2 islands: 5 + 2), helix, ramp (8 mm slot), plunge, lead arcs tangent per loop, conventional direction, errors and abort; the heightmap oracle ([`testing/region-coverage.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/testing/region-coverage.ts), S4's classification) on the star with 2 islands, the pocket with 2 islands at a clamped 0.9 D, the X0 enclosure cavity (Béziers flattened to lines until I1) and the 4 mm neck and 30° triangle: no gouge, no leftover, uncut = region − opening within 1 %. Goldens, the 30 random regions and the virtual-machine streaming stay with T3. Perf ([`region-pocket.perf.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-pocket.perf.ts)): the star with 4 levels and finishing in ≈ 9 ms (budget 500 ms), rest material ≈ 3 ms.

### Low entries and links (DF136)

Built 2026-11-27 for the pin-fin heatsink ([pattern-presets.md § Implementation (H2)](pattern-presets.md#implementation-h2)): every entry fed down from the retract plane at the plunge rate (G0 Z2, G1 Z(level above) F100), 3 851 times, 234 of its 453 min. The pocket now uses what its own levels above cleared; the first level is unchanged.

- **Low entries.** At a level whose levels above cut the same paths (the same ring tree and the same rest clipping from the first level cut; a rest pocket's switch to the whole tree below the roughing's floor starts from the retract plane again), every entry point lies on a path those levels cut (a ring, a helix circle, a rest run) or in the roughed air, so its column is clear down to the level above. The tool rapids there to the level above + `SLOT_CLEARANCE` (0.5 mm, the H1 slot rule's height) and only the last 0.5 mm is fed (plunge, ramp or helix, at their usual rates and angles). This covers the helix, ramp and plunge entries, blocked links and the rest pocket's run starts and air plunges.
- **Low links.** A blocked link (retract, rapid, plunge) of a plain pocket (no `restAfter`) crosses at the level above + the retract height (+ the corner radius: a bull nose leaves scallops up to it) instead of on the retract plane when both ends and the straight segment lie in the roots shrunk by the [safe-links](safe-links.md#link-profile) radial margin (1 mm): every point of the roots lies within reach of a ring (`clearingStep`), so the tool's disc + the margin along the link lies in what the levels above cleared. The same margins as the links between operations; never over uncut material (islands, pins, walls stand at full height and are outside that region). The tool holder is no lower than on the feed links the level already makes inside the same region.
- **Finishing.** A finishing loop whose lead start lies in the roots (cleared to the floor by every roughing level) rapids to its first finishing level + 0.5 mm (+ the corner radius) instead of plunging from the retract plane.
- **Unchanged:** the first level (entries from the retract plane), the lift to the retract plane at the end of each level, links between operations ([safe-links.md](safe-links.md), whose entry is still the first retract-plane descent), the slot rule, profiles.
- **Tests:** `testing/region-coverage.ts` `rapidClearance` sweeps the toolpath on a heightmap move by move and checks every rapid: none enters material, a rapid down below the retract plane ends ≥ 0.5 mm over the material in its column, a horizontal one passes ≥ the retract height over every cell within R + 1 mm (both less a cell). It runs in every `region-pocket.test.ts` case (with the roughing first for rest pockets), the region goldens, the 30 random regions and the inset fuzz, and on the pin heatsink (`heatsink-pins-toolpaths.test.ts`, also no gouge into a pin, and `airCut` plunges into material the same 192 as before at the golden's 2 mm stepdown: the leaves too small for a helix or ramp). Lowering the entries by 0.3 mm or the links by 1 mm, or allowing every low link, fails it. The rules' "rapids only on the retract plane" is `lowRapids` for pockets. The exported programs also replay through the safe-links oracle (`post/links-*.test.ts`).
- **Goldens:** 19 changed, by rapids only (every feed move identical, end point and rate; checked by parsing old and new): the four region goldens, `heatsink-pins-square.nc` (6 019 → 6 297 lines), the eight pattern goldens, `pocket-roughing.nc`, `shape-pocket-two-islands.nc` and the exit-criteria shapes 2 top, llm-first 2 top, clones-mirror handles and slotted countersink programs. Every other golden (the lid and enclosure programs among them) is byte-equal.

## Profile on general loops

Strategy parameters as [v0.1 profile](plan-v0.1.md#profile-with-tabs), plus `leadRadius` (length, default 0.5 D, ≥ 0; 0 = straight lead) for the finishing pass.

- **Passes:** distances from the shape as v0.1 (part side at R + allowance, then toward the offcut in steps ≤ `stepover`); each pass is `offset(loop, ±distance)` (outside: grows; inside: shrinks; an inside pass that vanishes in a neck splits, and each piece is cut).
- **`on`:** passes symmetric about the centreline; a slot equal to D is one pass on the loop or path. Closed loops keep the profile's climb direction for the outer passes; open paths zigzag by level (T-E) with ramps along the path.
- **Entry:** ramp along the first pass as v0.1; on an open path the ramp zigzags along the first stretch.
- **Tabs:** as v0.1 (lift where the tool disk overlaps a nominal tab band), with the general bands of [shapes.md](../architecture/shapes.md#geometry-for-shape-operations) at the design's positions; boundaries by sampling and bisection along arcs as along lines.
- **Input (K3):** `profileChain(op)` (the loop or open path in the face frame) and `tabStretches(op)` (tab stretches along it from its start point; none on an open path or a blind groove) in [`outline.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/outline.ts).
- **Finishing:** the part-side pass at full depth (in levels of `finishStepdown` since [hardening DF76](plan-v0.1.md#finishing-defaults-hardening-df75-df76)), lead-in and lead-out arcs of `leadRadius` on the offcut side, starting and ending at the start point unless a tab is there (then mid-way to the next tab).

### Implementation (T2, region-level generator)

Done after K3's mapping, not yet wired: [`src/core/toolpath/region-profile.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-profile.ts) `generateRegionProfile(input, port, steps?) → AppResult<Toolpath>`, pure, `Region2dPort` passed in as T1. The operation is `{ chain, closed, side, slotWidth, onZ, depth, through, tabs: { positions, width, thickness } }`: `chain`/`closed` from `profileChain(op)` turned into the setup's WCS (a mirroring turn that makes it clockwise is fine), `positions` = `tabCentres(length, op.tabs)` (the centres of `tabStretches`), `through` = no `op.depth`. The strategy is the v0.1 profile's plus `leadRadius`. Tab bands: [`geometry/tab-bands.ts`](../../src/core/geometry/tab-bands.ts) (shared with G1's solid tabs); the tab lift is v0.1's `liftOverTabs`. Choices the bullets above leave open:

- **Spacing:** `profileStep` = min(`stepover`, R) (a blind groove: the flat radius), so passes cover any loop by the pocket's argument; v0.1's analytic shapes allow 2R (√2·R inside rectangles). `profileStepClamped` tells the wiring when to add the T-H note. Pass distances as v0.1: `rowPositions(R + allowance, max(R, slotWidth − R))` from the offcut side.
- **Direction:** the region-on-left loops of each offset, reversed for climb when the part lies inside that offset (outside passes, the inner side of an `on` loop, its centreline), for conventional otherwise (inside passes, the outer side of `on`, the rings round an open path) – v0.1's rule (outside climb clockwise, inside climb counter-clockwise) for any loop orientation.
- **`on` closed:** per level the centreline, then pairs at ±d (offset ±d of the loop) out to slotWidth/2 − R − allowance; finishing rings at both walls. **`on` open:** the centreline from the end nearest the tool, alternating per level without a retract; a wider slot adds closed rings round the path (`offsetPath` by d, retract after the level) and finishing ring(s) of the band. The ramp walks the path from the end at `rampAngle`, turning back at the ends as often as needed (`zigzag`), then returns flat to the end and cuts the whole path at the level, so the level is complete without a clean-up pass. Ramps need a path or ring ≥ D long, else plunge (as T1).
- **Links** between rings: a straight feed inside the roughing's tool-centre band (T1's exact test), else retract, rapid, plunge (T-I). Closed profiles retract after each level (v0.1).
- **Inside covering slot:** if `offset(loop, slotWidth)` is empty the slot covers the opening: passes beyond the loop's inradius are empty and skipped, no tabs (v0.1). A piece too narrow for the allowance is roughed at the wall without finishing (v0.1 clamp); an inside tool up to `TOOL_FIT_TOLERANCE` too wide is accepted.
- **Tabs:** the band of a stretch is the stretch offset across the slot, one loop per smooth run and a sector at each sharper corner where the normals diverge ([shapes.md](../architecture/shapes.md#geometry-for-shape-operations), `geometry/tab-bands.ts`, shared with G1), not bent on the medial axis: near concave corners closer than the slot width it reaches past the "nearest point on the stretch" rule. Tool disk test exact against these line/arc loops; lifts by v0.1 sampling and bisection. Start point: arc length 0 unless a tab (width/2 + R) reaches it, then mid-way to the next tab.
- **Leads:** quarter circles of `leadRadius`, then ½, ¼, tangent at the start point on the offcut side, accepted when 16 samples of each lie in the slot's tool-centre region; else straight leads of the allowance; else none. Lead-in and lead-out use the tangents at the ring's start and end (a ring may start at a corner).
- **Errors:** `model.invariant` for the chain (`checkChain` rules, `noArea`, `empty`), `openNeedsOn`, `tabsNotAllowed`, `tabOutside`, `tabsOverlap`, tab thickness `range`, the v0.1 numeric rules and `leadRadius` ≥ 0; `plan.check` `toolTooLarge` (slot or opening), `noFlat` (wide blind groove or step without a flat). Since hardening DF53 any side may be blind (a step, [design-v0.3.md § Steps](design-v0.3.md#steps-blind-side-profiles-df53)): `outside`/`inside` passes stop at the depth like a blind groove's.
- **Tests** ([`region-profile.test.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-profile.test.ts)): pass distances by offset, inside neck split, direction (climb/conventional, clockwise chain), ramp/plunge, lead arcs tangent and fallbacks, tabs by arc length on lines and arcs (lift spans width + D on a line), `tabCentres` count tabs, `on` closed and open (zigzag, short-path zigzag ramp, wide slot rings), errors, abort; the heightmap oracle (`regionCoverage` with tab cells) on the X0 enclosure outline (imported by `importSvg`, outside with 3 tabs and finishing), its cavity (inside, uncut = slot − swept within 1 %), the bracket outside/inside/`on` with tabs and an open-path blind groove: no gouge, no leftover, tabs kept. Perf ([`region-profile.perf.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/region-profile.perf.ts)): the outline outside 14 mm slot with 4 tabs and finishing ≈ 18 ms, `on` 12 mm ≈ 19 ms (budget 500 ms). Goldens with T3.
- **Not switched:** v0.1 rectangles and circles (`outside` / `inside`, `count` tabs) keep `generateProfile`; the wiring step kept them ([Wiring](#wiring-t1-t2)).

### Wiring (T1, T2)

The plan side of both generators ([`toolpath/inputs.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/inputs.ts), [`request.ts`](../../src/core/toolpath/request.ts), [`plan/shape-findings.ts`](../../src/core/plan/shape-findings.ts)):

- **Which generator.** `toGeneratorInputs` sends an operation that `isAnalyticOperation` accepts (a rectangle with rotation 0 or a circle; a profile `outside` / `inside`, through, with `count` tabs) to the v0.1 generator, so v0.1/v0.2 plans keep byte-identical toolpaths and G-code (the goldens are unchanged; the input mapping is already byte-identical, [shapes.md § Implementation (K3)](../architecture/shapes.md#implementation-k3)). Every other pocket or profile – named or rotated shapes, `on`, blind, tab positions – becomes a `regionPocket` / `regionProfile` request. Decision of the wiring step: keep v0.1 for rectangles and circles; moving them (cap R instead of √2·R / 2R, lead arcs, other ring order) would change every v0.1 golden for no gain at the default stepover.
- **Frame.** The region (`pocketRegion`) or chain (`profileChain`) stays in the operation's face frame, like every other generator input; a rotated setup turns the finished toolpath (`wcs` on the request, `turnToolpath`, arc sense kept), so the mapping is the v0.1 `toWcs` turn. A mirrored shape is already placed (and renumbered) by K3, so the generator gets a valid loop either way.
- **Profile operation** (`regionProfileOperation`): `onZ` 0 (the stock face), `depth` = the blind groove's or step's depth, else the stock thickness with `through`; tab centres = `tabCentres(length, op.tabs)` on a closed through loop only (`tabStretches`' rule), else none. `leadRadius` has no plan parameter yet: the documented default 0.5 D.
- **Worker.** The request schemas carry the region/loop (`RegionSchema`, `LoopSchema`); `generateRequest(request, steps, region2d)` takes the worker's `TaskControl.region2d` for the two region kinds and fails with `internal.unexpected` without it. Steps and abort are the generators' own (`op.aborted` at the next step).
- **Findings** (`plan.warning.list`, with `services.region2d`): `plan.restMaterial` (warning, `field: tool`, `params { operation, area, u, v }` in the face frame) for every piece `findRestMaterial` finds in a general pocket with the plan's tool radius; `plan.stepoverClamped` (info, T-H, `field: stepover`, `params { operation, stepover, effective }`, "stepover clamped to 3 mm (half the tool) for offset clearing of pocket1") when `stepoverClamped` / `profileStepClamped` clamp the resolved stepover. Plain v0.1 shapes get neither. Neither blocks generation.
- **Shape checks in plan commands** (K3 known issue): `planFindings`, `planOperationInputs` (generate, get, export, the apply job, pending), the stock re-import and the revert now pass the port to the part check (`checkPart` in `plan/validity.ts`), so `profileOneLoop`, `tabOutside`, `tabsOverlap` and `tabsFit` run there as in the design. Since T3 the rebuild and blocker checks (`build.ts` `checkPartFits`, `rebuildBlocker`, `libraryUpdateBlocker`, through `planStale` and the pending changes) get it too ([Implementation (T3)](#implementation-t3)).
- **Stale.** The toolpath `inputHash` already hashes the plan's whole part, shapes included. `plan.stale.list` now marks an operation `changedInDesign` when the definition of the named shape it places differs between the plan's taken part and the design (a shape-only edit); `partChanged` lists `shapes` as before.
- **Tests** ([`plan/shape-toolpaths.test.ts`](../../src/core/plan/shape-toolpaths.test.ts)): a named-shape pocket and an `on` profile with tab positions generate through the job and export with G2 and G3 of ≤ 180°; `plan.restMaterial` in a 4 mm neck and both clamped-stepover notes at a 4.5 mm stepover, not blocking; a shape-only edit makes the draft's toolpath stale and a settled plan's operation `changedInDesign`; abort at a step; the X0 drawing (`importSvg`, loops turned into `path` items) pocketed and profiled with 3 tabs through plan export.

## Arcs in, G2/G3 out

- Every offset element is a `line` or `arc` move; arcs over 180° are split (v0.1 rule). Helices (ramp and helix entries along arcs) are `arc` moves with a Z change.
- **Degenerate arcs:** sweep < 1e-4 rad → `line`; radius < 0.005 mm → `line` (the offset of a corner arc just above zero). There is no radius rule in the toolpath: a huge radius is not a deviation bound (a 225 m arc over 300 mm bows 0.05 mm, [shapes.md § Arc fitting](../architecture/shapes.md#arc-fitting)).
- **Huge-radius arcs:** whether a G2/G3 of huge radius goes out as `G1` (GRBL computes in float32, so km radii lose precision in `I`/`J`) is the **post's** decision, beside its short-chord rule ([grbl.md § Output dialect](../architecture/grbl.md#output-dialect)); added with T3: an arc whose sagitta is at most half a resolution step is one `G1`, and an arc of radius over 10 m goes out as `G1` chords within 0.001 mm.
- **GRBL arcs:** the post already writes arcs as GRBL reads them (`I`/`J` from the written start, short chords as `G1`, the `error:33` end-on-circle guard, [grbl.md § Output dialect](../architecture/grbl.md#output-dialect)). v0.3 adds only the flat and huge-radius rule above; it feeds the post many more arcs, so the post's property tests gain the shape goldens (tiny corner arcs, near-180° splits, helices along arcs) in mm and inch.

## Plan side

- **Design snapshot:** carries the part's shapes; plan operations of shape operations group as today (equal holes only). Plan-only edits per T-F through `plan.design.operation.update`.
- **Tool checks** ([plan-v0.1.md § Tool assignment](plan-v0.1.md#tool-assignment-and-checks)): "wider than the pocket / slot / opening" uses the region's width (`offset(region, R)` non-empty); a corner radius below the tool radius is a warning (the corner keeps the tool radius), not an error, as today. The helix check of a general pocket compares the helix with the region's narrowest width.
- **Order checks** (`plan/order.ts`) use the placed region's bounding box where they used the shape's.
- No change to setups, export per setup, Run.

### Implementation (T3)

- **Tool checks** ([`plan/shape-checks.ts`](../../src/core/plan/shape-checks.ts), with `services.region2d`): for a general-shape pocket or an `inside` / `outside` profile (not `on`), placed by `placeShape` and measured by K4's `shapeMeasure` (cached per region): a tool that does not fit (`fits(R − FIT_SLACK)` false) is a `plan.check` error, `field: tool`, rule `pocketSize` (pocket) or `openingSize` (inside profile), "tool diameter must fit the pocket of pocket1 (4.99 mm at its widest)", `params { operation, diameter, pocket | opening }` (the narrowest width); it blocks generation like the v0.1 size rules and drops the tool from the suggestions. A named shape whose smallest inside corner is below R − 0.01 is a `plan.check` **warning**, rule `cornerRadius`, "… that corner keeps the tool radius" (never blocks); a rotated rectangle keeps the v0.1 corner error. A set `helixDiameter` with a helix entry that does not fit (helix + D > the narrowest width) is an error on `strategy.helixDiameter`, rule `fits`, `params { max }`. Rectangles and circles at rotation 0 keep their v0.1 rules. The findings run in `evaluateOperation` (plan evaluation, not the design's), so `plan.check`, `plan.tool.suggest`, `resolveTool` and the design tool findings see them. The renderer words them from their params in the project's units ("The tool (6.00 mm) is too wide for the opening: it is 4.99 mm at its narrowest.", `findingText` in [`messages/plan.ts`](../../src/renderer/src/messages/plan.ts)), and names `openingSize` as a design tool check ("is too wide for the opening"); `plan.stepoverClamped` reads "Clamped to 3.00 mm (half the tool) for offset clearing." in the project's units there too (v0.4 BL2).
- **Order checks** (`plan/order.ts`): `loosePiece` (which operations lie inside a profile) uses the placed shape's bounding box for a general shape (circles keep their radius test); without the port a general shape has no box and is skipped, as before.
- **Blockers with the port.** `rebuildPlan` (`BuildContext.region2d`), `rebuildBlocker`, `libraryUpdateBlocker` and `planStale` take the port, so a design edit that makes a general shape too small for its tool blocks the rebuild as for a rectangle.
- **T-F** for general shapes: `plan.design.operation.update` moves a named shape (`center`), re-points it to another shape of the snapshot (`shapeId`, `input.malformed` for an unknown one) and changes depths; the snapshot's shapes are never edited by the plan (there is no plan shape command), and the operation shows `editedInPlan`.
- **Helix search.** `deepestPoint` treats an offset the 2D library returns invalid just before the region collapses ("an offset hole lies in no outer loop", met on a turned gear among the random regions) as empty, so the bisection goes on and the radius stays a lower bound.
- **Blind `on` grooves (found by X1).** A blind groove is checked at its `depth` below the stock face, without breakthrough: the plan's flute and stickout checks (`plan/checks.ts`, wording "groove depth must be…"), the design's reach rule and tool fit (`reachDepth` in `geometry/tool-fit.ts`: `dfm.reach`, the auto choice), and `plan.cutoutBeforeSetup` skips it (a groove frees nothing; `plan/setups.ts`). Before, a 1 mm groove on the bottom of a 40 mm board failed the 25 mm flute and warned that it cut through before the top setup.
