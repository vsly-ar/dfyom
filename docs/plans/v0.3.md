## Exit criteria

The test part is the user's reference `enclosure-wood-base.stl` ([X1 of v0.2](v0.2.md#6-acceptance)), recreated **with its real outline** instead of rectangles. Measured from the STL: 249.5 × 177 × 39 mm; an oval outline with no straight sides (close to a superellipse of exponent 2.5); an oval cavity 235 × 159.4 mm (walls 7.2 mm at the sides and front, 10.5 mm at the back) down to a 4.2 mm floor; four Ø ≈ 3 mm blind holes ≈ 3.9 mm deep in the bottom at (±88.5, ±44.3) mm from the centre; a ≈ 4 mm bevel round the bottom edge. The bevel needs a chamfer operation (after v0.3, [features.md](../modes/features.md)) and is left out. A test-only SVG of the two sections (`enclosure-sections.svg`, cubic Béziers, generated once from the STL and committed) stands in for the drawing a user would have ([Q27](#confirmed-decisions); built by task X0).

- **In the app** (the golden path, [X2](#7-acceptance)): new project, 255 × 185 × 40 mm board; import the STL reference and fit it; **Import SVG…** the sections: the report shows two shapes, Béziers fitted to arcs and the deviation; snap a point to the reference section and measure the back wall; in the Shape Editor build a **cable notch** from an obround and a rectangle (a boolean) and rotate it; operations: facing on top; the cavity as a pocket on the facing floor; the notch as a pocket across the back wall; the outline as an outside profile with four tabs placed along the path and a locked library tool; four drillings on the bottom; an `on` groove along an open path on the bottom; Apply. Plan derives two setups (bottom, top); generate; the top program has `G2`/`G3` along the oval; export per setup; run both on the virtual machine. Save, close, reopen: shapes, placements, tabs unchanged.
- **The same flow headless** through MCP and `dfyom batch` ([X1](#7-acceptance)), including `design.shape.import` and `design.shape.add`, with golden programs; and a **deviation check** (test-only): the evaluated part's section at mid-wall height lies within 0.2 mm of the reference's section (outline and cavity).
- ADR 0019 accepted; the spike fixtures run as 2D port regression tests.
- `npm run check` and the e2e suite green in CI.

## Known issues and risks

- **`tools/list` size** is 94.9 K of the 100 K budget after K2 (108 tools); K3 and I1 must share item schemas between `add`/`update` or the budget must be raised ([llm-api.md](../architecture/llm-api.md)). After K3: 97.7 K, 110 tools; `$defs` are per tool, so `design.shape.import` must fit in 2.3 K.
- **Until G1/T1/T2 wiring (K3):** parts placing a named or rotated shape, or a profile that is `on`/blind/with tab positions, fail evaluation with `shapeSolidLater` and toolpaths refuse them with `shapeToolpathLater`.
- **K3 gaps for later tasks:** parameter DFM and tool-fit skip general shapes (K4); plan `loosePiece` order and helix checks skip them (T3); plan commands run without the 2D port, so `profileOneLoop`/`tabOutside`/`tabsOverlap`/`tabsFit` are skipped there; plan stale indicators miss shape-only edits; the renderer draws no outline for named/rotated shapes (U1/U2); a blind `on` groove has no `profile.floor` surface yet (G1).
- **2D port, non-tangent concave arcs:** growing a hand-made region whose concave arcs meet a larger arc at an angle can fail in the crate; the port now returns `resultInvalid` instead of an empty region (the collapsed-arc case found in T1 is fixed by stepped offsets). Shrinks are not cross-checked.
- **D30 GRBL `$$` import — done** (outside the v0.3 lanes, user-approved): paste import (`library.machine.settings.preview`/`.import`, Library window "Import $$…"), machine `$11`/`$12`/steps per mm, shared `$$`parser with the handshake. Follow-up after the serial transport: diff on real-machine connect ([library.md § GRBL`$$` import](../architecture/library.md#grbl--import)).
- **D30 Recommended presets — done** (presets follow the feeds-and-speeds calculator until edited, `library.tool.add/update`, Library window checkbox); follow-up: refresh them when calculator data changes (today on the tool's next save). **Settings shortcut** Ctrl/Cmd+, — done.
- **SVG import semantics:** the elements of one shape combine even-odd (a circle inside an outline becomes a hole) and fill colours / `fill="none"` are ignored; `use`, `switch` (first child only) and CSS `<style>` sheets are not supported (I1 core).
